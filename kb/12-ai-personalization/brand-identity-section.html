Brand Identity Section

The Brand Identity section is the first half of the Personalization tab. It has two fields: Brand Name and Brand About. Together they tell the AI who you are at the most basic level. Skipping or under-filling these fields is the most common reason AI output reads generic.

Brand Identity section with Brand Name and Brand About fields

Filling the Brand Identity Fields

1
Brand Name
A single line free-text field for your official brand name (e.g. "7 Seers"). Use the exact spelling and capitalization you want to appear in AI-generated copy. If your brand has a stylized form (e.g. all caps, mixed case), enter it the way you write it in marketing materials. The AI will use this name verbatim in meta descriptions, alt text, and schema fields.
Brand Name input field with example value
2
Brand About
A multi-line description of what the business does, who it serves, and what makes it different. This is the most important text field in all of RankFrame. The AI quotes from it, paraphrases it, and uses its tone as a model for generated copy. A weak Brand About (one sentence, generic adjectives) produces weak AI output. A strong Brand About (3 to 5 sentences, specific, opinionated) produces strong AI output.
Brand About textarea with multi-line description
3
What Makes a Strong Brand About
Three things: (a) what you do (the actual service or product), (b) who you serve (specific audience descriptors), (c) what is unique (your edge, philosophy, or proof). Avoid vague claims like "we deliver excellence". Replace them with concrete capabilities like "we ship Framer landing pages in 14 days with built-in SEO".
4
Brand About Length and Format
Aim for 50 to 200 words. Use line breaks to separate ideas. Bullet points are fine if your tool supports them but plain paragraphs work just as well. The AI does not care about formatting: it cares about the content. A focused 80-word Brand About beats a rambling 400-word version.
5
Examples of Effective Brand About Copy
See the contrast below. The generic example uses safe but useless language. The strong example names the service, the audience, and the differentiators in plain sentences.
Generic example (avoid)
"We are a creative studio that builds amazing websites for our clients. We care about quality and design."
Strong example (preferred)
"7 Seers builds Framer websites for SaaS founders. We design for conversion, not just looks. Every site ships with full schema, optimized speed, and a 7-day SEO audit. Based in Lucknow, working with founders globally."

Tips for Writing Brand About

  • Include your service noun (websites, agency, course, app).
  • Name your customer type (SaaS founders, local clinics, indie creators).
  • Mention one or two differentiators (speed, niche, proof).
  • Mention location if relevant to the audience or SEO strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I write Brand About in a different language?
Yes. The AI handles non-English input. Generated copy will reflect the language of your Brand About. If you want English output but your brand description is in Hindi, write it in English here.
How often should I update Brand About?
Update it whenever your positioning changes meaningfully (new service line, new audience, repositioning). Small wording tweaks are fine but not necessary.
Will the AI quote my Brand About verbatim?
Sometimes phrases will appear in generated copy. The AI uses Brand About as context and tone reference, paraphrasing more often than quoting. Avoid putting confidential details in this field.
Is there a maximum length?
No documented limit. Keep it focused. Beyond 250 words, the AI starts losing focus on your core positioning.
My brand has multiple sub-brands. How do I handle that?
Use the parent brand name in Brand Name and describe the sub-brand structure inside Brand About. If sub-brands have very different audiences, consider separate RankFrame accounts.
