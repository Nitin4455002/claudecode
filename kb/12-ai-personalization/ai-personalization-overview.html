AI Personalization in RankFrame

Personalization is what separates RankFrame's AI from a generic ChatGPT prompt. Before any AI feature in the plugin generates a meta title, description, alt text, or schema field, it reads the data you saved in the Personalization tab. That data tells the AI who you are, who you serve, and what you sell. The result is AI-generated copy that sounds like your brand from the first run, not generic boilerplate you have to rewrite.

Personalization tab inside Account Settings, showing Brand Identity and Strategy sections.

How Personalization Works

1
Where Personalization Lives
Open Account Settings (click your avatar block at the bottom of the left sidebar), then click the Personalization tab at the top. The tab is one of two: Profile and Personalization. It is also shown automatically the first time you connect a new Framer site to RankFrame, before any AI feature can run.
Account Settings sidebar with Profile and Personalization tabs at the top.
2
The Two Sections of Personalization
Personalization has two distinct sections stacked vertically: BRAND IDENTITY (your name and what you do) and STRATEGY (your goals, industry, audience, and competitors). Both sections feed the AI. Filling out only one section gives partial context. Filling both unlocks the most accurate AI output.
Brand Identity and Strategy sections inside the Personalization tab.
3
Why Personalization Exists
AI without context produces generic copy. A meta description for a Framer studio that just says "Our team builds beautiful websites" is forgettable. The same AI with Personalization data produces "Premium Framer-built websites for SaaS founders by 7 Seers, a Lucknow-based studio." That specificity is what helps your pages rank for the right queries and convert the right visitors.
4
What AI Features Use This Data
Every AI generation in RankFrame reads Personalization: AI meta title (Global and per-page), AI meta description (Global and per-page), AI alt text (Image SEO single and bulk), AI schema field suggestions (Auto-Generate flow), and AI keyword and content recommendations. There is no AI feature in RankFrame that ignores Personalization.
5
Save Settings Button
At the bottom of the Personalization tab is a full-width purple Save Settings button. Click it whenever you change any field. The data persists across sessions and applies to every site connected under your account. Changes take effect on the next AI generation: no restart required.
Full-width purple Save Settings button at the bottom of the Personalization tab.

Generic vs Personalized Output

Generic (no Personalization) "Welcome to our studio. We build websites that help businesses grow."
Personalized (filled) "7 Seers builds Framer websites for SaaS founders ready to scale. Lucknow-based, design-led, conversion focused."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Personalization required to use RankFrame?
Personalization is required for AI features to produce brand-specific output. You can use non-AI features (manual schema, 404 monitor, indexing) without filling Personalization, but you should fill it before relying on any AI generation.
Does Personalization apply to all my connected sites?
Personalization is account-level. The same data applies across every site connected to your RankFrame account. If you manage multiple unrelated brands, consider creating separate accounts for clean separation.
Can I edit Personalization later?
Yes. Open Account Settings and click the Personalization tab any time. Update any field and click Save Settings. Future AI generations use the updated data.
Where is the data stored?
Your Personalization data is stored on RankFrame servers and used as context for AI generation. It is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. See the privacy policy at rankframe.com/privacy.
I skipped Personalization during onboarding. Can I add it now?
Yes. Open Account Settings, click Personalization, fill the fields, and click Save Settings. The AI engine will use it on every generation from that point forward.
