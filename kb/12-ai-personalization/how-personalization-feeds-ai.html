How Personalization Powers AI Features

Every "Write with AI" or "Generate" button in RankFrame is connected to your Personalization data. This article walks through each AI feature in the plugin, explains what data it uses, and shows realistic before-and-after examples comparing generic AI output (Personalization empty) with branded AI output (Personalization filled).

Personalization tab inside Account Settings, with Brand Identity and Strategy sections visible

AI features connected to Personalization

1 AI Meta Title (Global Settings Basic Tab and Per-Page View)

The "Write with AI" button next to the AI Generated Title field generates a 60 to 70 character title. It reads Brand Name, Brand About, and Industry from Personalization. With Personalization empty, output looks like "Welcome to Our Website Home Page". With Personalization filled (7 Seers, SaaS, Agency), output becomes "7 Seers, Premium Framer SEO Studio for SaaS Brands".

Write with AI button next to AI Generated Title field in Global Settings Basic tab
2 AI Meta Description (Global Settings Basic Tab and Per-Page View)

The "Write with AI" button next to the AI Generated Description field generates a 150 to 160 character description. It reads Brand About, Audience, and Main Goals. With Personalization empty: "Our team builds great websites for businesses worldwide." With Personalization filled (Generate Leads goal, SaaS Founders audience): "RankFrame builds conversion-focused Framer websites for SaaS founders. Schema, speed, and SEO baked in. Start your trial today."

AI Generated Description field with Write with AI button
3 AI Alt Text (Image SEO, Single and Bulk)

The wand icon on each image and the bulk Generate Alt Text button generate descriptive alt text using Personalization. Brand Name appears in branded portfolio shots, Industry tone applies across all images. Without Personalization: "Image of a person at a computer". With Personalization filled (Photography Industry): "7 Seers founder reviewing client wireframes during a Framer design sprint."

Image SEO panel with wand icon on individual image and bulk Generate Alt Text button
4 AI Schema Field Suggestions (Auto-Generate Flow)

When you click Auto-Generate in the Advanced Tab, the AI fills schema fields (Headline, Description, Image URL, Author Name, etc.) using Personalization. Brand Name populates the Publisher field. Brand About context shapes the Description. Without Personalization, schemas use generic placeholder phrasing. With Personalization filled, schemas reflect your actual brand voice.

Advanced Tab Auto-Generate button with schema fields populated
5 AI Keyword and Content Recommendations

Future RankFrame AI features for content recommendation will read Personalization to suggest topics, gaps, and improvements aligned with your audience and goals. Filling Personalization now positions you to benefit from these features as they ship.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI also read my live page content?
Yes. AI features combine Personalization with the actual page content (headings, body text, image filenames). Personalization provides the brand context. The page content provides the topic context. Both signals shape the output.
If I update Personalization, do existing AI-generated outputs update automatically?
No. Existing saved outputs stay saved until you regenerate them. To refresh outputs after a Personalization update, click Generate or Write with AI again on each field you want refreshed.
How many credits does AI generation cost?
1 credit per generation. Each meta title, meta description, alt text, or schema field generation uses 1 credit. Bulk image alt text on 100 images uses 100 credits.
Can I see exactly what data the AI is using?
The AI reads your Personalization fields and the relevant page or image data. Personalization data is visible in Account Settings, Personalization. There is no per-generation log of the exact prompt used.
What happens if my Personalization is partially filled?
AI will still generate output, but missing fields produce more generic results in those dimensions. Brand Name empty means no brand reference in copy. Goals empty means no conversion bias. Fill all fields for the strongest AI output.
