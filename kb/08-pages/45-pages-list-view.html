Pages List View

The Pages list is your command center for every URL on your Framer site. Find a page in seconds, see how it scores, and know when it was last crawled.

Opening the Pages list

Click the database icon in the RankFrame left sidebar. The Pages section opens with the subtitle Manage your page settings in Framer. Every Static page and CMS entry on your site is listed here, grouped into folders that match your Framer structure.

Pages list with folders expanded showing Static and CMS labels

Anatomy of the list

The list view has five things to know.

1
Refresh button

The Refresh button sits at the top of the Pages section. Use it after publishing in Framer or after adding new pages so RankFrame pulls a fresh page list and re-reads metadata.

2
Search bar

The Search bar filters every page instantly across every folder. Type any part of a title or URL and the list narrows in real time. There is no submit button, results update as you type.

3
Collapsible folders

Folders mirror your Framer site structure, for example Insaaf, Projects, or Thoughts. Each folder header shows a type label of Static or CMS and a page count badge so you can see size at a glance. Click the header to collapse or expand.

4
Page row

Each row shows the full page title, its URL, the current SEO score, and the last crawled date. Click anywhere on the row to open the per-page split view and start editing.

5
Score color dots

The colored dot next to each score makes triage fast.

  • Purple dot: 85 and above. The page is in great shape.
  • Orange dot: 70 to 84. There are quick wins to capture.
  • Red dot: below 70. Open this page next.

Suggested workflow

  1. 1
    Press Refresh
    Make sure the list reflects what is currently published in Framer.
  2. 2
    Scan for red dots
    Collapse healthy folders and focus on pages scoring below 70 first.
  3. 3
    Open a page
    Click any row to enter the split view and start fixing issues.
  4. 4
    Use search for re-checks
    After publishing changes, search the page title and confirm the score moved up.
Tip

Keep folders collapsed for sections that already score purple. The list stays scannable and your eye goes to the work that matters.

Note

Last crawled dates only update after RankFrame audits a page. If a date looks old, open the page or press Refresh to trigger a fresh read.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open the Pages list?
Click the database icon in the RankFrame left sidebar. The Pages section opens with the subtitle Manage your page settings in Framer.
What does each color dot mean on a page row?
A purple dot means the SEO score is 85 or higher. Orange means 70 to 84. Red means below 70 and needs attention.
How are pages grouped?
Pages are organized into collapsible folders that mirror your Framer site structure. Each folder shows whether it contains Static or CMS pages and a count badge.
Does the search bar work across folders?
Yes. The search bar filters every page on your site instantly, regardless of which folder the page lives in.
When should I press Refresh?
Press Refresh after publishing changes in Framer or adding new pages so RankFrame pulls the latest list and re-reads metadata.
