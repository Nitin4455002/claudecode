Why per-page titles matter

Search engines show your title as the clickable headline and your description as the snippet underneath. Google often truncates titles around 60 characters and descriptions around 160, so length matters as much as wording. These fields are per-page, separate from the site-wide defaults you set in Global Settings Basic.

Per-page title and description fields with character counters and AI helpers

The four fields and two buttons

1
Current Title

The Current Title field shows the page's current title with a live character counter. RankFrame warns you when the title goes over 70 characters because Google typically truncates around 60.

2
AI Generated Title

The AI Generated Title field holds an AI suggestion. Use the Generate button to produce one and the Copy button to put it on your clipboard. The AI uses your Personalization data so the tone matches your brand, audience, and industry.

3
Current Description

The Current Description field shows the page's current meta description with a character counter. The recommended sweet spot is 150 to 160 characters. RankFrame warns when you go over 165, the point where Google is likely to cut you off.

4
AI Generated Description

The AI Generated Description field works just like the title version. Press Generate for a new suggestion and Copy to grab the text. The output is shaped by your brand identity in Personalization.

5
Copy button

The Copy button copies the AI-generated text to your clipboard. From there you can paste it into Framer or anywhere else you keep marketing copy.

6
Generate button

The Generate button produces a fresh AI suggestion. Each generation uses 1 credit. If you do not love the first result, generate again until the suggestion fits.

Suggested workflow

  1. 1
    Read the current title
    Check the counter. If it is over 70, you have a clear win available.
  2. 2
    Generate an AI title
    Press Generate. Review the suggestion against your target keyword.
  3. 3
    Copy and paste into Framer
    Use the Copy button, then paste into the page settings in Framer.
  4. 4
    Repeat for the description
    Aim for 150 to 160 characters. Stay under 165 to avoid truncation.
  5. 5
    Republish and refresh
    Publish in Framer, then press Refresh in RankFrame to see the new score.
Tip

Put the most important words first. Google may truncate, but readers also scan the first few words to decide if your result is worth the click.

Note

If your Personalization data is empty, AI suggestions will be generic. Fill out brand identity, audience, and industry before generating for the best output.

Frequently asked questions

What is the recommended title length?
Aim for under 60 characters so Google does not truncate the title in search. RankFrame warns when your current title goes over 70 characters.
What is the recommended description length?
Target 150 to 160 characters. RankFrame warns when the current description goes over 165 characters since Google often truncates around 160.
How does the Generate button work?
Generate produces an AI-suggested title or description. It uses 1 credit per generation and pulls your brand identity, audience, and industry from Personalization to keep copy on brand.
How is this different from Global Settings Basic?
These fields apply to a single page only. Global Settings Basic sets the site-wide default title and description used when a page does not have its own.
Does Copy paste the suggestion into Framer?
Copy puts the AI-generated text on your clipboard so you can paste it where you need it, including back into Framer page settings.
