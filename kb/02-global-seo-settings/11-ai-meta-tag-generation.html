Using AI to Generate Meta Tags in RankFrame

RankFrame's Write with AI button generates keyword-rich meta titles and descriptions in seconds. The output quality depends on how well you set up Personalization.

What Write with AI does

The Write with AI button appears next to your meta title and description fields in the Basic tab. Click it to generate a fresh, keyword-rich version in seconds.

The AI is not a generic ChatGPT prompt. It is tuned for SEO copywriting and pulls context from your Personalization data: brand name, business description, goals, industry, target audience, and competitors.

That means the output sounds like your brand, not a template.

Why Personalization matters

If your Personalization data is empty or vague, the AI has nothing to work with. It will generate generic copy that could apply to any business.

If your Personalization data is detailed, specific, and current, the AI generates copy that is tailored to your exact brand and audience.

Tip

Before using Write with AI for the first time, fill in every field in the Personalization tab. The 5 minutes you spend there pay off across every AI generation forever. See Managing Your RankFrame Account.

How to use it

  1. 1
    Confirm Personalization is filled in
    Click the account block in the sidebar. Open the Personalization tab. Make sure Brand Name, Brand About, Industry, and Audience are all filled in.
  2. 2
    Open Global Settings, Basic tab
    Click Global Settings in the sidebar. The Basic tab is selected by default.
  3. 3
    Click Write with AI
    Click the Write with AI button next to the title or description field. RankFrame generates a fresh version in seconds. The output appears in the AI-Generated column on the right.
  4. 4
    Edit or regenerate
    Edit the AI output to match your voice, or click Write with AI again to generate another option. Each regeneration uses 1 credit.
  5. 5
    Copy and save
    Click the Copy button next to the AI output to put it on your clipboard, or paste it directly into the title or description field. Click Save to apply.
  6. 6
    Republish your Framer site
    Open Framer and republish your project. The new meta tags appear on your live site after the publish completes.

Credit usage

Each AI generation uses 1 credit.

  • Generating a meta title once: 1 credit
  • Generating a meta description once: 1 credit
  • Regenerating either: 1 credit per regeneration

Editing the AI output manually does not use extra credits. You only spend a credit when you click Write with AI.

If you run low, click Buy Credits in the Image SEO header to refill. See Plans and Pricing for credit pricing.

Note

Credits are non-refundable once used. Take a moment to refine your Personalization data before generating, so the first output is closer to what you want.

Frequently asked questions

How many credits does Write with AI use?
Each AI generation uses 1 credit. Generating a title costs 1. Generating a description costs 1. Generating both costs 2.
Why does my AI-generated meta sound generic?
AI quality is directly tied to your Personalization data. If your Brand About, Industry, or Audience fields are empty or vague, the AI has no context. Fill in the Personalization tab in detail and regenerate.
Can I regenerate a meta description?
Yes. Click Write with AI again. Each regeneration uses 1 credit. You can also edit the AI output manually without using extra credits.
Can I use AI generation on individual pages?
Yes. The same Write with AI feature is available in the Pages section for per-page meta tags. The AI considers the specific page content alongside your brand context.
Is the AI output original?
Yes. RankFrame uses generative AI to write fresh copy based on your brand context. Always review and edit AI output before publishing.
