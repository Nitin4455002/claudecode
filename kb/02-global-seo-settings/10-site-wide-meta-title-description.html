Why meta tags matter

The meta title is the clickable headline that appears in Google search results. The meta description is the short paragraph below it. Together, they decide whether someone clicks your result or scrolls past it.

They also signal to search engines what your page is about. A clear, keyword-relevant title and description help your page rank for the right queries.

For Framer sites, RankFrame is the easiest place to manage both. The Basic tab in Global Settings shows your current values side-by-side with AI-generated alternatives so you can compare and improve at a glance.

Character limits

  • Meta title: 70 characters maximum. Google displays the first 50 to 60 characters on most devices.
  • Meta description: 260 characters maximum. Aim for 150 to 160 to avoid truncation.

RankFrame shows a live character counter as you type so you always know how close you are to the limit.

How to set them in RankFrame

  1. 1
    Open Global Settings
    Open RankFrame in Framer and click Global Settings in the left sidebar.
  2. 2
    Make sure you are on the Basic tab
    The Basic tab is selected by default. It shows two columns: your current live title and description on the left, AI-generated alternatives on the right.
  3. 3
    Write or paste your meta title
    Type your title in the title field. Watch the character counter to stay under 70. The counter turns red if you exceed the limit.
  4. 4
    Write your meta description
    Type a description between 150 and 160 characters. Make it specific, benefit-focused, and include your primary keyword.
  5. 5
    Save your changes
    Click Save. The values are stored in RankFrame immediately.
  6. 6
    Republish your Framer site
    Open Framer and republish your project. The new meta tags appear on your live site after the publish completes.
Tip

If you are not sure what to write, click Write with AI. RankFrame uses your Personalization data (brand name, audience, industry) to generate keyword-rich copy in seconds. See Using AI to Generate Meta Tags.

Best practices

For meta titles

  • Lead with your primary keyword
  • Include your brand name at the end (e.g. "...| RankFrame")
  • Make it specific to the page, not generic to your industry
  • Use natural language, not stuffed keywords
  • Stay under 70 characters

For meta descriptions

  • Open with the most important benefit or value statement
  • Include your primary keyword naturally
  • End with a call to action ("Try free", "Learn more", "Get started today")
  • Keep it between 150 and 160 characters
  • Make it match the search intent for your target keyword

Frequently asked questions

What is the recommended character limit for a meta title?
Keep it under 70 characters. Google typically displays the first 50 to 60 characters in search results. RankFrame shows a live character counter as you type.
What is the recommended character limit for a meta description?
Aim for 150 to 160 characters. Google often truncates descriptions longer than that in search results. The hard limit in RankFrame is 260 characters.
Will my Global meta title appear on every page?
The Global meta title is your default. It applies to every page that does not have a per-page override. You can override it for specific pages from the Pages section.
Why does Google show a different meta description than the one I set?
Google sometimes generates its own description based on page content if it considers your meta not relevant enough. Make your description specific to your page and include your primary keyword to reduce this.
Do I need to republish my Framer site after changing meta tags?
Yes. Changes save to RankFrame immediately, but new meta tags only appear on your live Framer site after you republish the project from Framer.
