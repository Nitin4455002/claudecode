Reading the Keyword Analysis Dashboard

RankFrame's Keyword Analysis section shows you exactly how often each keyword appears on your page and how dense each one is. This article shows you how to read the numbers and act on them.

What the dashboard shows

The Keyword Analysis section sits below your saved keywords inside Global Settings, Keywords tab. It analyzes your homepage's content and shows you a breakdown of every word and phrase RankFrame finds.

This is different from your Saved Keywords list. Saved Keywords is what you want to rank for. Keyword Analysis is what your page actually contains.

Comparing the two reveals gaps. If a keyword you want to rank for does not appear often enough on the page, RankFrame surfaces that mismatch.

Word count filter

The word count filter lets you focus on phrases of a specific length.

  • 1 word: single words like "design", "framer", "seo". Often dominated by filler words.
  • 2 words: two-word phrases like "framer sites", "ai schema", "alt text". More meaningful for SEO.
  • 3 words: three-word phrases like "framer seo plugin", "image alt text", "google indexing api". Usually the most actionable for keyword targeting.
Tip

Default to the 2-word and 3-word filter when reviewing your dashboard. Single-word data is dominated by filler words like "and" and "the" that do not affect ranking.

What to do with the data

Find density gaps

Search for your saved focus keyword in the table. If it appears with a density under 0.5%, your page does not feature it strongly enough. Edit your page in Framer to use the keyword more naturally in headings and body content, then republish and recrawl.

Spot keyword stuffing

If any keyword shows density above 5%, you may be over-using it. Soften the language by varying word choice and using synonyms. Search engines penalize keyword stuffing.

Discover new opportunities

Browse the 2-word and 3-word lists for phrases with high frequency. These are terms your content already emphasizes. They might be worth saving as additional focus keywords, especially if they have decent search volume.

Note

The Keyword Analysis section in Global Settings reflects your homepage. For per-page analysis, open the Pages section and click any individual page to see its own Keyword Analysis breakdown.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy keyword density?
There is no single correct number, but 0.5% to 2% is healthy for your primary keyword. Density above 3% to 5% can look like keyword stuffing.
What does Total Frequency mean?
Total Frequency is the count of all keyword instances on the page, including filler words. It is mostly useful as the denominator for density calculations.
Why do common words like "and" and "to" show up as top keywords?
RankFrame counts every word on the page. Filler words naturally appear most often. Use the word count filter (1, 2, 3 words) to focus on meaningful multi-word phrases.
How do I increase a specific keyword's density?
Add the keyword naturally to headings, the first paragraph, image alt text, and body content. Avoid forcing it. Update in Framer, republish, then run Recrawl Website in RankFrame.
Is Keyword Analysis available for every page?
Global Settings shows data for your homepage. For per-page analysis, open the Pages section and click any page to see its own Keyword Analysis.
