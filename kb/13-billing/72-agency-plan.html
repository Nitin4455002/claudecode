Agency Plan

The Agency plan is the most popular choice for design studios and agencies managing multiple client websites on Framer. At $40 per month (or $288 per year), it connects unlimited Framer sites under a single subscription and includes every RankFrame feature plus priority support. If you manage two or more client sites, the Agency plan costs less per site than buying multiple Personal plans.

Price

The Agency plan costs $40 per month on a monthly subscription, or $288 per year on an annual subscription. Annual billing works out to $24 per month, a 40% saving over the monthly rate.

  • Monthly: $40 billed each month, cancel any time
  • Annual: $288 billed once per year, equivalent to $24/month
  • Saving: 40% discount on annual versus monthly

For a studio managing even two Framer sites on the annual plan, the cost works out to $12 per site per month. That is less than one Personal plan at $9/mo per site on annual.

Unlimited Sites

Connect as many Framer sites as your agency manages under a single Agency subscription. There is no cap, no extra charge per site, and no approval process for adding new sites. Your workflow for site management looks like this:

  • Add a new client site when you onboard them to Framer
  • Remove old sites when projects close to keep your dashboard clean
  • Switch between connected sites from inside the RankFrame plugin

Each site operates independently. Adding a tenth site does not slow down or affect the other nine.

Priority support covers questions about RankFrame features, plugin setup, Framer integration, billing changes, and site transfer requests.

Who It Is For

The Agency plan is built for anyone who manages more than one Framer site professionally. Common fits include:

  • Design studios with 2 or more ongoing Framer client sites
  • Agencies with a Framer-first tech stack and recurring SEO retainers
  • Freelancers who manage retainer clients and want to include SEO reporting as a service deliverable
  • SaaS teams managing multiple Framer landing pages across regions or markets
  • Growth agencies running Framer for clients across different industries

If you currently have a Personal plan and your client base is growing, upgrading to Agency is a one-click change in the billing portal.
