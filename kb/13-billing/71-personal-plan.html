Personal Plan

The Personal plan is designed for freelancers, indie hackers, and anyone managing a single Framer site. At $15 per month (or $108 per year), it includes every RankFrame feature with no capability restrictions. The one limitation is the site count: one connected Framer site per Personal subscription.

Price

The Personal plan costs $15 per month on a monthly subscription, or $108 per year on an annual subscription. Annual billing works out to $9 per month, a 40% saving over the monthly rate. Annual billing is the best value if you plan to maintain your Framer site long-term.

  • Monthly: $15 billed each month, cancel any time
  • Annual: $108 billed once per year, equivalent to $9/month
  • Saving: 40% discount on annual versus monthly

Site Limit

The Personal plan supports exactly one connected Framer site. Once you connect a site to your Personal account, that license is bound to that site. You cannot self-transfer to a different site from inside the plugin.

If you need to change your connected site, email hello@7seersmedia.com. The 7 Seers team reviews and handles the transfer manually. If you are regularly managing more than one Framer site, consider the Agency plan instead.

Free Trial

The Personal plan starts with a 7-day free trial. No credit card is required to begin the trial. You get full feature access from day one, with the following trial-specific soft limits:

  • URL submissions capped at 2
  • Schema pages visible capped at 3
  • Site recrawls limited to 1
  • Starter AI credits included at no charge

All limits lift immediately the moment you upgrade to a paid Personal plan. No waiting period, no manual action needed.

Who It Is For

The Personal plan is a strong fit for anyone running a single Framer site who wants complete SEO tooling without paying for agency-level capacity. Common use cases include:

  • Freelancers running a personal portfolio on Framer
  • Designers with one client site they maintain full-time
  • SaaS founders managing their own product landing page
  • Bloggers or indie makers who publish on Framer
  • Developers building and maintaining a single Framer project

If your work expands to two or more client Framer sites, upgrade to the Agency plan. The Agency plan at $40/mo becomes cheaper per site than two Personal plans at $30/mo combined.
