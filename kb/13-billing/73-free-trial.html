Free Trial

Both the Personal and Agency plans include a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. You get full access to the plugin from the moment you install it. A small set of trial limits are in place to protect the platform from abuse, but every core workflow is usable within them. All limits lift the instant you upgrade to a paid plan.

RankFrame plugin open on a fresh trial account showing full feature access from day one
1
Trial Duration

Your 7-day trial starts from the date you connect your first Framer site to RankFrame. No credit card is required to start. You have full access to every feature from day one. The trial countdown is shown in the sidebar so you always know how many days remain.

RankFrame sidebar footer showing trial countdown badge and "See Billing" upgrade button
2
Image SEO

There is no usage limit on viewing or editing alt text manually during the trial. AI alt text generation uses credits. Your trial includes approximately 500 starter credits at no charge. When your credits run out, open the Buy Credits modal from the Image SEO header to top up. Credit purchases are available at any point during or after the trial.

Image SEO panel showing the credit balance badge and Buy Credits purple button in the header
3
Automatic Schema

During the trial, the Auto-Generate Schema flow shows a maximum of 3 pages. You can view, generate, and edit schemas for those 3 pages in full. Pages beyond the first 3 are hidden until you upgrade. This is enough to validate schema output quality before committing to a paid plan.

Automatic Schema panel during trial showing 3 pages available and an upgrade prompt for additional pages
4
404 Monitor

There is no trial limit on 404 monitoring. All 404 errors are tracked from day one. You can enable the monitoring script immediately and start collecting broken link data right away. All error records are preserved when you upgrade.

404 Monitor panel showing active error tracking during the trial period
5
Recrawl Website

You get 1 recrawl during the trial. The Recrawl Website button (shown in red/coral in the sidebar) triggers a fresh crawl of your Framer site to pick up new pages and content changes. Use your single trial recrawl after you have made significant changes so you get the most useful snapshot. Paid plans include unlimited recrawls.

RankFrame sidebar with the Recrawl Website button highlighted, showing the 1-use trial indicator
6
Submit Indexing

During the trial you can submit up to 2 individual URLs to Google via the Submit URLs tab. This is enough to verify the indexing pipeline is working correctly for your site. Sitemap pushes count separately and are also available during the trial. Paid plans remove the submission limit entirely.

Submit Indexing panel showing the Submit URLs tab with the 2-URL trial limit indicator
7
Total Pages

All pages on your Framer site are visible in the Pages section during the trial. There is no page count restriction. You can review SEO settings, set meta titles and descriptions, and configure canonical URLs for every page from the start.

Pages section showing all site pages visible and accessible during the trial
8
Upgrading

Click the See Billing button at the bottom of the RankFrame sidebar, or go to dashboard.rankframe.com to choose a plan. Billing is processed through Polar.sh. All trial limits lift immediately on successful payment. There is no waiting period and no need to reconnect your site.
