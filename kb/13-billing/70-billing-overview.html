Billing Overview

RankFrame offers two subscription plans and a separate AI Credits system. Plans cover access to all features. AI Credits are consumed each time the AI generates a meta title, description, alt text, or schema field. This article explains how the two billing streams work, where to manage them, and how to avoid running out of credits mid-project.

1
Two Plans: Personal and Agency

Personal costs $15 per month or $108 per year and covers one Framer site. Agency costs $40 per month or $288 per year and covers unlimited Framer sites. Both plans include every RankFrame feature with no capability restrictions. Choosing annual billing saves 40% on both plans compared to paying month to month.

  • Personal: $15/mo or $108/yr -- ideal for a single Framer site
  • Agency: $40/mo or $288/yr -- unlimited sites, priority support
  • Annual saves 40% on both plans
Plan selection with Personal and Agency toggle, monthly/annual pricing
2
AI Credits

AI Credits are billed separately from your plan subscription. One credit is consumed each time the AI generates a meta title, description, alt text, or schema field. You purchase credits directly inside the plugin through the Image SEO Buy Credits modal.

  • First-time offer: 30,000 credits for $49
  • Regular refill: 30,000 credits for $100
  • Free trial includes starter credits at no charge
  • Credits do not expire while your subscription is active
Image SEO Buy Credits modal showing credit packages and pricing
3
See Billing Button

The purple full-width "See Billing" button sits at the bottom of the RankFrame left sidebar. Clicking it opens the Polar.sh billing portal directly in your browser. From the portal you can:

  • View your active plan and next renewal date
  • Switch between monthly and annual billing
  • Update your payment method
  • Download invoices as PDF
  • Cancel your subscription
RankFrame left sidebar with the purple See Billing button at the bottom
4
Payment Processor (Polar.sh)

All billing is handled by Polar.sh, RankFrame's payment partner. When you click See Billing, you are redirected to your Polar.sh customer portal. Polar.sh processes card charges, manages subscription state, and stores billing records. You interact with Polar.sh directly for card updates, invoice downloads, and cancellation requests.

Polar.sh billing portal overview page showing plan, payment method, and invoice history
5
Free Trial

Both the Personal and Agency plans offer a 14-day free trial with credit card required. You get full feature access from day one with trial-specific soft limits. For example, URL submission is limited to 2 during the trial, schema pages are capped at 3, and recrawls are limited to 1. All limits lift immediately the moment you upgrade to a paid plan.

Trial upgrade prompt inside RankFrame showing remaining trial days
6
Renewal Countdown

Your current plan name and renewal date are always visible at the bottom of the RankFrame sidebar without needing to open the billing portal. For example, you might see "Agency Yearly Plan, Renews in 357 days." This lets you track your billing cycle at a glance while working inside the plugin.

RankFrame sidebar footer showing plan name and renewal countdown
