Managing Your RankFrame Account

Most account management for RankFrame happens at dashboard.rankframe.com, not inside the Framer plugin. The plugin shows your account info for reference, but editing your name, email, password, and billing all happen via the external dashboard and billing portal. This article covers where to go for each type of account action.

Account Actions and Where to Take Them

Use the numbered blocks below to locate the right place for each type of account task. A quick-reference table at the end of this section summarizes all six actions at a glance.

1 View Account Info in the Plugin Profile Tab
To check your current account details without leaving Framer, click the avatar block at the bottom of the left sidebar to open Account Settings, then select the Profile tab. You will see your avatar, display name, plan badge, email address, join date, and authentication method. All fields here are read-only and shown for reference only.
Account Settings modal open on the Profile tab, showing read-only account fields
2 Update Name, Email, or Password
To change your display name, email address, or password, open a browser and go to dashboard.rankframe.com. Sign in with your current credentials and navigate to your profile or account settings. After saving, the updated information will appear in the plugin the next time you open Account Settings.
If you signed up with Google Auth, password changes must be handled through your Google account settings, not through the RankFrame dashboard.
dashboard.rankframe.com profile settings page showing editable name, email, and password fields
3 Change Your Billing Plan
To upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your RankFrame subscription, click the See Billing button in the sidebar account block. This button is purple and always visible at the bottom of the left sidebar. It opens the Polar.sh billing portal in your browser, where you can manage your subscription, view invoices, and update payment details. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
Sidebar account block with the purple See Billing button highlighted
4 Change Your Connected Site (Personal Plan)
The Personal plan supports one connected Framer site. If you need to change which site is linked to your account, you cannot do this from inside the plugin or the dashboard self-service portal. Send an email to hello@7seersmedia.com with your account email address and the URL of the new site you want to connect. The support team will update your account and confirm when done.
Sidebar showing the connected site URL and the support contact reference
5 Sign Out of RankFrame
To end your current RankFrame session, open Account Settings by clicking the avatar block in the left sidebar, then go to the Profile tab. Scroll to the bottom of the tab and click the Sign Out button (full-width, dark red). Your session ends immediately and you are returned to the login screen. You will need to sign in again to access your data.
Profile tab with the full-width dark red Sign Out button visible at the bottom
6 Contact Support
For any account issue not covered by the self-service options above, reach the 7 Seers support team at hello@7seersmedia.com. A small status dot in the sidebar indicates whether the team is currently online. When the dot is green, the team is active and response times are typically fast. When it is not green, the team is offline and will reply when they are back.
Sidebar showing the support status dot and the contact email reference

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I update my name or email address for RankFrame?
Visit dashboard.rankframe.com, sign in, and edit your profile details there. Changes are reflected in the plugin the next time you open Account Settings.
How do I upgrade or downgrade my RankFrame plan?
Click the See Billing button in the sidebar of the RankFrame plugin. This opens the Polar.sh billing portal where you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription.
Can I connect a different website to my Personal plan?
The Personal plan supports one connected site. If you need to change which site is connected, email hello@7seersmedia.com and the support team will assist you.
How do I sign out of RankFrame?
Open Account Settings by clicking the avatar block in the left sidebar, then go to the Profile tab and click the Sign Out button at the bottom of the tab. This ends your current session and returns you to the login screen.
How do I contact RankFrame support?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com at any time. The sidebar in RankFrame also displays a small status dot indicating whether the support team is currently online, so you can gauge expected response time before reaching out.
