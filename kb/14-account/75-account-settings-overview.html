Account Settings Overview

Account Settings is a modal that floats over the plugin panel. It contains two tabs: Profile (your identity and plan details) and Personalization (the brand and strategy data that feeds every AI feature). You open it any time by clicking your avatar block at the bottom of the left sidebar.

How to Open Account Settings

The Account Settings modal is always one click away. You do not need to navigate away from whatever screen you are working in inside RankFrame.

1 The Avatar Block (Sidebar Trigger)
Look at the bottom of the left sidebar in the RankFrame plugin panel. You will see a block displaying your avatar circle, name, and email address. Click anywhere on this block to open the Account Settings modal. The modal opens on top of the plugin without navigating away from your current view.
Avatar block at the bottom of the left sidebar, showing avatar circle, name, and email
2 The Account Settings Modal
Once open, the modal displays two tabs at the top: Profile and Personalization. Click either tab label to switch between them. Close the modal by clicking outside it or using any dismiss control to return to your plugin view.
Account Settings modal open, showing the Profile and Personalization tab labels at the top

The Two Tabs at a Glance

Each tab serves a distinct purpose. Understanding which tab controls what will save you time when you need to find or update something.

3 Profile Tab
The Profile tab shows your account identity and plan status. This includes your avatar, display name, plan badge (Free Plan, Personal, or Agency), email address, join date, authentication method, and a Sign Out button. All fields here are read-only inside the plugin. To edit your name, email, or password, visit dashboard.rankframe.com.
Profile tab showing avatar, name, plan badge, email, join date, and Sign Out button
4 Personalization Tab
The Personalization tab is where you enter the brand, business, and content strategy data that RankFrame uses to power its AI features. This includes your brand voice, target audience, business description, and content guidelines. Unlike Profile, these fields are fully editable inside the plugin. Keep them up to date as your strategy evolves.
Personalization tab showing brand and strategy input fields

The Sidebar Account Block

The sidebar account block sits at the bottom of the left sidebar at all times. It is visible across every screen inside RankFrame and gives you a permanent summary of your account without opening the modal.

5 Always-Visible Account Summary
The sidebar block shows: your avatar circle, your display name, your email address, your current plan tier, a renewal countdown timer, a See Billing button (purple) that opens your billing portal, and a Recrawl Website button (red/coral) that triggers a fresh crawl of your connected site. This information is for reference only from the sidebar.
Sidebar account block showing avatar, name, email, plan tier, renewal countdown, See Billing button, and Recrawl Website button
6 Support Status Indicator
A small dot in the sidebar indicates whether the 7 Seers support team is currently online. When the dot is green, the team is available. You can reach the team at hello@7seersmedia.com regardless of the indicator state.
Close-up of the support status dot in the sidebar
Editing profile details: The Account Settings modal is a read-only reference for your Profile information. To update your name, email, or password, visit dashboard.rankframe.com. Billing changes are handled through the portal linked from the See Billing button in the sidebar.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open Account Settings in RankFrame?
Click the avatar block at the bottom of the left sidebar inside the RankFrame plugin panel. The Account Settings modal opens immediately on top of the plugin without navigating away from your current view.
What are the two tabs inside Account Settings?
Account Settings has two tabs: Profile and Personalization. Profile shows your identity and plan details. Personalization holds the brand voice, business, and strategy data that powers every AI feature in RankFrame.
Which fields inside Account Settings can I edit?
All fields in the Profile tab are read-only inside the plugin. To change your name, email, or password you need to visit dashboard.rankframe.com. Fields in the Personalization tab are fully editable inside the plugin.
What is the difference between Account Settings and the sidebar account block?
The sidebar account block is always visible at the bottom of the left sidebar and shows a quick summary: your avatar, name, email, plan tier, renewal countdown, a See Billing button, and a Recrawl Website button. Account Settings is a modal you open deliberately to view full details or edit Personalization data.
How does Account Settings relate to dashboard.rankframe.com?
The Account Settings modal inside the plugin is a read-only reference for your profile information. Any changes to your name, email, password, or billing plan must be made at dashboard.rankframe.com or through the billing portal linked from the sidebar.
