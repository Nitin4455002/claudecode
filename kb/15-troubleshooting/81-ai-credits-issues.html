Troubleshooting

Fixing AI Credit Issues

AI Credits power every generation in RankFrame. When something goes wrong with credits (balance not showing, generation failing, purchase not reflecting), the fix is usually quick. This article covers every credits-related symptom and its resolution.

Before You Run a Bulk Generation

Before bulk-generating, check your credit balance. Multiply the number of selected images by 1 to estimate the credit cost. Make sure your balance covers the full batch before you start.

1 Balance Shows 0 and You Cannot Generate
CauseYour AI credits are fully exhausted.
FixFixClick the Buy Credits button in the Image SEO header and purchase a refill. The balance updates immediately after a successful purchase.
Image SEO header showing credits badge at 0 and the Buy Credits button
2 You Purchased Credits but Balance Did Not Update
CausePayment succeeded but the sync between Polar.sh and RankFrame is momentarily delayed.
FixFixClick the refresh icon inside the credits badge in the Image SEO header. If the balance still does not update after 5 minutes, contact hello@7seersmedia.com with your Polar.sh receipt.
Image SEO header credits badge with the refresh icon highlighted
3 AI Generation Produces Blank or Very Short Output
CauseThe Personalization tab is empty or nearly empty, giving the AI no brand context to work from.
FixFixOpen Account Settings > Personalization tab. Fill in Brand Name, Brand About, and at least two Strategy fields. Then retry the generation.
Account Settings > Personalization tab showing Brand Name, Brand About, and Strategy fields
4 Bulk Alt Text Stopped Mid-Generation
CauseCredits ran out partway through a bulk run. Images processed before credits were exhausted will have saved alt text; the remaining images were not processed.
FixFixCheck the credits badge balance, buy a refill, then reselect the images that did not generate and run Generate Alt Text again.
Image SEO bulk generation view with unprocessed images remaining after credits ran out
5 Generation Fails with an Error Message
CauseA network issue or temporary API outage interrupted the generation request.
FixFixWait 2 to 3 minutes and retry. If the error persists, check that the plugin has a network connection and that you are not behind a VPN or firewall blocking external API calls.
Plugin showing an error message on a failed generation attempt
6 Credits Were Consumed but Generation Produced Wrong Output
CauseCredits are consumed when a generation runs, regardless of output quality. This is expected behavior by design.
FixFixYou can regenerate (this costs one additional credit) to get better output, or edit the AI output manually in the alt text field. Improving your Personalization settings before regenerating usually produces significantly better results.
Image SEO alt text field showing AI-generated output available for manual editing
ⓘ Credits are tied to your RankFrame account and are non-transferable. If you have a billing concern not covered above, email hello@7seersmedia.com with your Polar.sh order ID.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when credits run out during a bulk alt text generation?
The bulk generation stops at the point where credits are exhausted. Images processed before the credits ran out will have their alt text saved. Images that were not yet processed will remain unchanged. Purchase additional credits, then reselect the unprocessed images and run Generate Alt Text again.
Are credits refunded when an AI generation fails due to an error?
Credits are consumed when a generation request is sent to the API, regardless of whether the output is usable. Failed generations caused by network errors or API outages are not automatically refunded. If you believe credits were consumed incorrectly due to a platform-side error, contact hello@7seersmedia.com with the date and approximate time of the failed generation.
How do I check my credit purchase history?
Credit purchases are processed through Polar.sh. Log in to your Polar.sh account at polar.sh to view your full order history and receipts. You can also find purchase confirmation emails in the inbox associated with your account email.
Can I transfer AI credits to another RankFrame account?
No. AI credits are tied to the account that purchased them and cannot be transferred to a different RankFrame account. Credits are also non-refundable once consumed.
How long does a credit purchase take to appear in my balance?
In most cases the credit balance updates immediately after a successful payment. If the balance has not updated after 5 minutes, click the refresh icon inside the credits badge in the Image SEO header. If the balance still does not reflect your purchase after 5 minutes, contact hello@7seersmedia.com with your Polar.sh receipt.
