Troubleshooting

Fixing AI Credit Issues

AI Credits power every generation in RankFrame. When something goes wrong with credits (balance not showing, generation failing, purchase not reflecting), the fix is usually quick. This article covers every credits-related symptom and its resolution.

Symptoms and Fixes

1 Balance Shows 0 and You Cannot Generate
Your credits are exhausted. No AI generations can run while the balance is at zero.

Fix: Click the Buy Credits button in the Image SEO header and purchase a refill. The balance updates immediately after a successful payment.
Credits badge showing 0 with the Buy Credits button highlighted in the Image SEO header
2 You Purchased Credits but Balance Did Not Update
Payment succeeded but the sync between Polar.sh and RankFrame is sometimes delayed by a few seconds.

Fix: Click the refresh icon inside the credits badge in the Image SEO header. If the balance still does not update after 5 minutes, email hello@7seersmedia.com and include your Polar.sh receipt so the team can apply the credits manually.
Refresh icon inside the credits badge, Image SEO header
3 AI Generation Produces Blank or Very Short Output
The AI uses your Personalization settings to tailor output. When Personalization is empty or sparse, the AI has nothing to work with and may produce minimal results.

Fix: Open Account Settings > Personalization tab. Fill in your Brand Name, Brand About, and at least two Strategy fields. Save your changes, then retry the generation.
Account Settings Personalization tab with Brand Name, Brand About, and Strategy fields visible
4 Bulk Alt Text Stopped Mid-Generation
A bulk run consumes one credit per image. If your balance reaches zero before the batch finishes, the run stops at that point.

Fix: Check the credits badge balance. Purchase a refill using the Buy Credits button. Then reselect only the images that did not receive generated alt text and run Generate Alt Text again.
Image SEO bulk selection with unprocessed images highlighted, Buy Credits prompt visible
Tip: Plan ahead for bulk runs

Before bulk-generating, check your credit balance. Multiply the number of selected images by 1 to estimate the credit cost. Purchase enough credits before starting so the entire batch completes in one run.

5 Generation Fails with an Error Message
An error message during generation usually points to a temporary network issue or a brief API outage on the server side.

Fix: Wait 2 to 3 minutes and retry. If the error continues, confirm that the plugin has a working network connection and that you are not behind a VPN or corporate firewall that blocks external API calls. If the error persists beyond 10 minutes, contact hello@7seersmedia.com.
Error message state in the Image SEO generation panel
6 Credits Were Consumed but Output Was Wrong
Credits are consumed at the moment a generation request is sent. This happens regardless of whether the output meets your expectations. This is expected behavior.

Options: You can run the generation again (this costs one additional credit) to get a different result. Alternatively, edit the AI-generated output manually directly in the alt text field. Improving your Personalization settings often produces better output on the next attempt.
Alt text field with AI-generated output ready for manual editing
