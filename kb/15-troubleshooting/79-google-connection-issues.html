Fixing Google Connection Issues

RankFrame connects to Google via OAuth to enable Submit Indexing (URL pushes, Analytics, Keyword Tracker, and Indexing History). If that connection breaks or was set up with the wrong account, none of these features work. This article walks through every connection symptom and how to fix each one.

Connection Symptoms and Fixes

1 Submit Indexing Shows "Not Connected" or Fails to Load Data
SymptomSubmit Indexing displays "Not Connected", the panel spins indefinitely, or all data fields are blank.
CauseYour OAuth token has expired or was revoked through Google Account security settings.
FixOpen Submit Indexing, go to the Setup tab, click the red Disconnect link to clear the expired token, then click Connect Google Account and sign in again.
Submit Indexing Setup tab showing "Not Connected" state and the red Disconnect link
2 Wrong Google Account Connected
SymptomThe plugin shows a connected account, but Analytics shows zeros, keyword data is empty, and domain verification fails.
CauseYou signed in with a personal Gmail account instead of the Google account that owns the Search Console property for your domain.
FixGo to Submit Indexing > Setup tab, click Disconnect, then reconnect using the exact Google account that has Owner or Full User access to your domain in Search Console.
Setup tab showing the connected account email address and Disconnect option
3 Domain Verification Shows Failed or Pending
SymptomThe Domain Verification step in the Setup tab shows a failed or pending status even after connecting a Google account.
CauseThe connected Google account does not have Search Console access to the domain RankFrame is trying to verify.
FixGo to Google Search Console, open your property, go to Settings > Users and permissions, and confirm your connected account is listed as Owner or Full User. Then return to RankFrame, disconnect, and reconnect that account.
Setup tab showing Domain Verification step with a failed status indicator
4 Analytics Data Shows Zeros or "No Data"
SymptomThe Analytics tab in Submit Indexing shows all zeros or a "No data available" message immediately after connecting.
CauseA new OAuth connection needs 48 to 72 hours to pull historical data from Search Console. The property may also not have accumulated enough impressions or clicks yet.
FixWait 48 to 72 hours after connecting, then reload the Analytics tab. If data still does not appear after 72 hours, disconnect and reconnect, then wait again.
Analytics tab showing loading state and expected data layout after population
5 Indexing History Shows Errors on Every Submission
SymptomEvery URL in the Indexing History shows an error status, even URLs that were previously submitted successfully.
CauseGoogle's Indexing API quota was exceeded (typically 200 requests per day). Batch-submitting a large sitemap at once is the most common way this happens.
FixWait 24 hours for the daily quota to reset. When you resubmit, prioritize your most important or recently updated pages rather than submitting every URL at once.
Indexing History tab showing error states alongside the submission date and URL columns
Quota Tip

Google's standard Indexing API quota is 200 requests per day per project. Submitting your entire sitemap at once uses that quota immediately. Submit new and updated pages in small batches to avoid exhausting it.

How to Reconnect Your Google Account

Follow these steps to fully disconnect and reconnect a Google account in RankFrame.

  1. 1 Open the RankFrame plugin in Framer and click on the Submit Indexing panel in the sidebar.
  2. 2 Click the Setup tab at the top of the Submit Indexing panel.
  3. 3 Click the red Disconnect link next to the currently connected account. Confirm the disconnection if prompted.
  4. 4 Click Connect Google Account. A Google sign-in window will open in your browser.
  5. 5 Sign in with the Google account that has Owner or Full User access to your Search Console property. Do not use a generic Gmail account.
  6. 6 Grant all requested permissions when prompted. RankFrame needs Search Console read access and Indexing API write access.
  7. 7 Return to the Setup tab. Confirm the Domain Verification step now shows a green checkmark. If it shows pending, wait one to two minutes and refresh.
Seven-step reconnect flow: Setup tab, Disconnect click, Google sign-in window, and verified connection state
Which Account Should I Use?

Use the Google account you logged into when you set up your Search Console property. If you are not sure which account that is, open Search Console at search.google.com/search-console and check which account owns the property for your domain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my Google OAuth connection expire in RankFrame?
Google OAuth tokens expire after a period of inactivity or when you revoke access through your Google Account security settings. When this happens, RankFrame loses its ability to communicate with Search Console and the Indexing API. To fix it, open Submit Indexing, go to the Setup tab, disconnect the current account, and reconnect with your Google account.
What happens if I connect the wrong Google account to RankFrame?
If you connect a Google account that does not have access to the Search Console property for your domain, domain verification will fail, no Analytics data will load, and URL submissions will be rejected. Disconnect the account in the Setup tab and reconnect using the Google account that has Owner or Full User access to your Search Console property.
What level of Search Console access does my Google account need?
Your connected Google account needs at least Full User access, though Owner access is recommended. Restricted User access does not grant the permissions required for the Google Indexing API. You can check and update access levels in Google Search Console under Settings, then Users and permissions.
How many Indexing API requests does Google allow per day?
Google's standard Indexing API quota is 200 requests per day per project. If you submit a large batch of URLs in a short period you may exhaust this quota, causing subsequent submissions to return errors. Wait 24 hours for the quota to reset, then resubmit in smaller batches. Prioritize your most important or recently updated pages.
What should I do if reconnecting Google still does not fix the issue?
If you have disconnected and reconnected the correct Google account and the issue persists, check that your Search Console property is set up as a Domain property (not a URL-prefix property) for the broadest API access. If it still does not work, contact 7 Seers support at hello@7seersmedia.com with your Framer site URL and a description of the error message you see.
