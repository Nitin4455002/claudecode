Getting Support for RankFrame

RankFrame is built and supported by 7 Seers, a design and software studio based in Lucknow, India. The team is reachable directly by email and monitors the plugin's built-in support indicator. This article explains how to reach the team, what to include in your message, and what to expect in terms of response time.

Before You Email
  • 1Check this help center. Most feature questions are answered in the documentation.
  • 2Try the fix described in the relevant troubleshooting article.
  • 3Recrawl the site using the Recrawl Website button if data looks stale.
  • 4Reconnect Google in the Submit Indexing Setup tab if indexing is failing.
  • 5If still stuck, email hello@7seersmedia.com with the details listed in step 4 of this article.
1 Support Email

hello@7seersmedia.com is the primary channel for all support requests, billing questions, account issues, and bug reports.

Include your account email, a description of the issue, and any screenshots or screen recordings that help explain the problem. The more detail you provide, the faster the team can diagnose and resolve your issue.

Email client composing a support request to hello@7seersmedia.com
2 Support Indicator in the Plugin

The Support button at the bottom of the left sidebar (next to Documentation) has a green dot when the team is online. Click the Support button to initiate a conversation or open the support interface.

The green dot signals that a team member can respond quickly. When the dot is absent or grey, you can still send a message and the team will respond on the next business day.

RankFrame left sidebar showing the Support button with a green online indicator dot
3 Agency Plan Priority Support

Agency plan subscribers receive priority responses. If you are on the Agency plan and waiting longer than expected, mention your plan tier in your support message. This helps the team route your request to the front of the queue.

RankFrame billing screen showing the Agency plan tier indicator
4 What to Include in a Support Message

Including the right information upfront saves back-and-forth. When you email support, provide the following:

  • ›Your account email address
  • ›Plugin version (visible in the RankFrame panel)
  • ›Your Framer site URL
  • ›Description of the issue (what you expected vs. what happened)
  • ›Steps to reproduce the issue
  • ›Screenshots or screen recordings if relevant
RankFrame panel footer showing the plugin version number
5 Billing and Account Issues

For billing portal questions, plan changes, and refund requests, email hello@7seersmedia.com.

All billing is processed through Polar.sh. Reference your Polar.sh order ID when asking about a specific payment so the team can locate your transaction quickly.

Polar.sh order confirmation showing the order ID to reference in support emails
6 Personal Plan Site Transfer

Disconnecting or switching the connected site on a Personal plan requires emailing hello@7seersmedia.com. This is the only account action that requires manual team intervention. Include your account email and the site URL you want to connect in your message.

RankFrame connected site display showing the current Personal plan site
7 Documentation and Help Center

The Documentation button (book icon) in the left sidebar links to the RankFrame help center at rankframe.com/help.

Check documentation first. Most feature questions, setup steps, and troubleshooting scenarios are covered in detail there. The help center is updated alongside every major plugin release.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for RankFrame support to respond?
The 7 Seers team typically responds within one business day. Agency plan subscribers receive priority responses. You can check whether the team is currently online by looking for the green dot next to the Support button in the RankFrame sidebar. When the dot is green, responses are often faster.
How do I report a bug in RankFrame?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com with a subject line that starts with "Bug Report". Include your Framer site URL, the RankFrame plugin version, a description of the steps to reproduce the issue, and any error messages shown in the plugin. Screenshots or screen recordings are especially helpful for UI bugs.
What should I do if I don't hear back from support within two business days?
Send a follow-up email to hello@7seersmedia.com referencing your original message. Check your spam folder to confirm the team's reply did not land there. If you are on the Agency plan, mention your plan tier in the follow-up to ensure priority handling.
Is there a community forum or Discord for RankFrame users?
There is no public community forum or Discord at this time. The primary support channel is email at hello@7seersmedia.com. Check the help center at rankframe.com/help for documentation on all features.
How do I request a new feature for RankFrame?
Send your feature request to hello@7seersmedia.com with a description of what you would like to see and how it would benefit your workflow. The 7 Seers team reviews all feature requests and considers them when planning future updates.
