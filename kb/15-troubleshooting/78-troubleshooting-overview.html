Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Overview

Most RankFrame issues fall into a small set of categories. This overview maps the most common symptoms to the right fix or article. Before contacting support, run through the checklist below: most issues resolve in under five minutes.

Common Issue Categories

1 Google Connection Not Working

Symptom: Submit Indexing shows "Not Connected", Analytics data is blank, or the Keyword Tracker fails to load.

Cause: Your OAuth token has expired or you connected the wrong Google account.

Fix: Open the Submit Indexing panel, go to the Setup tab, click the red Disconnect link, then reconnect with the correct Google account. See the full guide: Fixing Google Connection Issues.

Submit Indexing panel with Setup tab open and Disconnect option visible
2 Indexing Not Submitting

Symptom: The Push URLs button is greyed out or submissions appear to fail silently.

Cause: Trial accounts are limited to 2 URL submissions. Domain verification may also be incomplete.

Fix: Open Submit Indexing and check the Setup tab for the Domain Verification step status. If you have reached the trial limit, click See Billing to upgrade. See the full guide: Fixing Indexing Issues.

Submit Indexing Setup tab showing domain verification status and billing button
3 AI Generation Not Working or Producing Blank Output

Symptom: Clicking Generate in Image SEO or meta fields produces nothing, a loading spinner that never finishes, or empty results.

Cause: Your AI credit balance has reached zero, or the Personalization tab is empty (no brand context for generation).

Fix: Check the credits badge in the Image SEO header. If credits are at zero, upgrade or purchase more. Open the Personalization tab and confirm your site name, brand description, and tone are filled in.

Image SEO header showing credits badge and Personalization tab with brand fields
4 404 Monitor Not Tracking Errors

Symptom: The 404 Monitor panel shows no errors even though broken links exist on your site.

Cause: The monitoring script toggle is off, or the domain listed in Allowed Domains does not match your live site domain exactly.

Fix: Go to the 404 Monitor tab, enable the tracking toggle, and verify the domain in Allowed Domains matches your published site URL (no trailing slash, correct subdomain).

404 Monitor tab with tracking toggle and Allowed Domains field highlighted
5 Schema Not Injecting

Symptom: Structured data does not appear in Google's Rich Results Test or the page source after enabling schema types.

Cause: The Schema Injection master toggle in Global Settings is off, or you enabled individual schema types without clicking Save and Inject afterward.

Fix: Go to Global Settings > Advanced tab, confirm the Schema Injection master toggle is on, enable the schema types you need, then click Save and Inject.

Global Settings Advanced tab with Schema Injection master toggle and Save and Inject button
6 Images Not Appearing in Image SEO

Symptom: The Image SEO panel is empty or shows far fewer images than you have published on the site.

Cause: RankFrame's image index is out of date. This happens when new pages were published after the last crawl.

Fix: Click the Recrawl Website button (the red/coral button in the plugin sidebar). RankFrame will re-fetch all pages and repopulate the image list within a few seconds.

Plugin sidebar with Recrawl Website button highlighted
7 Plugin Showing Stale Data

Symptom: Meta titles, descriptions, or page lists in RankFrame do not match what is currently published on your Framer site.

Cause: RankFrame caches your site data from the last crawl. If you have published changes in Framer since then, the plugin has not yet seen them.

Fix: Click Recrawl Website in the sidebar. This refreshes all pages, meta data, and image references.

Plugin sidebar highlighting Recrawl Website button after publishing changes in Framer
8 Can't Find a Feature

Symptom: You know RankFrame has a feature but cannot locate it in the interface.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most common causes of RankFrame not working?
The most common causes are an expired or incorrect Google OAuth connection, a domain that has not been verified in the Setup tab, AI credits at zero, a disabled 404 monitor toggle, or a Schema Injection master toggle that has never been turned on. Run through the checklist above to identify which category applies to your situation.
How do I report a bug in RankFrame?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com with the subject line "RankFrame Bug Report". Include your Framer project URL, a description of what you expected to happen, a description of what actually happened, and any error messages visible in the plugin or in the browser console (press F12 and open the Console tab).
What information should I include when contacting RankFrame support?
Include your Framer site URL, the RankFrame plan you are on (Trial, Personal, or Agency), a clear description of the issue, the steps you already tried, any error text shown in the plugin, and a screenshot if possible. The more context you provide, the faster the support team can identify the cause.
How long does RankFrame support take to respond?
The 7 Seers team typically responds within one business day. For urgent issues, check whether the support green dot in the plugin sidebar is lit, which indicates the team is currently online and available for a faster reply.
Will recrawling the website fix most data display issues in RankFrame?
Yes. If the plugin is showing stale page data, missing images in Image SEO, or outdated meta information, clicking the Recrawl Website button (the red/coral button in the sidebar) forces RankFrame to re-fetch your site content. Most display-related issues resolve immediately after a recrawl.
