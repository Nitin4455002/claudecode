Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Overview

Most RankFrame issues fall into a small set of categories. This overview maps the most common symptoms to the right fix or article. Before contacting support, run through the checklist below: most issues resolve in under five minutes.

1 Google Connection Not Working
CauseOAuth token expired or you are connected to the wrong Google account.
FixOpen Submit Indexing, go to the Setup tab, click the red Disconnect link, and reconnect with the correct Google account that has access to your Search Console property.
Submit Indexing > Setup tab showing the Disconnect and Connect buttons
2 Indexing Not Submitting
CauseURL submission limit reached on a trial account, or your domain has not passed verification.
FixOpen the Setup tab in Submit Indexing and check the Domain Verification step status. If you are on trial, upgrade via the See Billing button to remove submission limits.
Submit Indexing > Setup tab showing Domain Verification status and See Billing button
3 AI Generation Not Working or Producing Blank Output
CauseAI credits are at zero, or the Personalization tab is empty (no brand context provided).
FixCheck the credits badge in the Image SEO header. If credits are zero, purchase more from your billing page. If credits are available, open the Personalization tab and fill in your brand name, description, and target keywords.
Image SEO header showing the AI credits badge and Personalization tab
4 404 Monitor Not Tracking Errors
CauseThe monitor script toggle is turned off, or the domain listed in Allowed Domains does not match your live site domain.
FixGo to the 404 Monitor tab, enable the script toggle, and verify that your domain is entered correctly in the Allowed Domains field. Changes take effect after your next Framer publish.
404 Monitor tab showing the script enable toggle and Allowed Domains input
5 Schema Not Injecting
CauseThe Schema Injection master toggle is off, or you enabled individual schemas but did not click Save and Inject afterward.
FixOpen Global Settings and go to the Advanced tab. Turn on the Schema Injection master toggle, enable the schema types you need, then click Save and Inject. Republish your Framer site.
Global Settings > Advanced tab showing Schema Injection toggle and Save and Inject button
6 Images Not Appearing in Image SEO
CauseYour site has not been crawled recently, so RankFrame's image index is out of date.
FixClick the Recrawl Website button (the red or coral button in the sidebar). The crawl typically completes within one to two minutes, after which all site images appear in Image SEO.
RankFrame sidebar highlighting the Recrawl Website button
7 Plugin Showing Stale Data
CauseData displayed in the plugin (pages list, image inventory, audit results) reflects a previous crawl snapshot.
FixClick Recrawl Website in the sidebar. This refreshes all plugin data including the pages list, image inventory, and SEO Analyzer results in one pass.
Sidebar showing Recrawl Website button and last crawl timestamp

Still stuck?

If none of the fixes above resolve your issue, contact the 7 Seers support team at hello@7seersmedia.com. Look for the green support dot in the RankFrame sidebar: when it is green, the team is online and typically responds within a few hours. When it is grey, expect a response within one business day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most common causes of RankFrame not working?
The five most frequent causes are: an expired Google OAuth connection, a trial account that has reached its submission limit, AI credits reaching zero, the 404 monitor script toggle being off, and a schema injection master toggle that has not been turned on. Check each of these in order before contacting support.
How do I report a bug in RankFrame?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com with the subject line starting with "Bug Report". Include your Framer site URL, the RankFrame plugin version, a description of the steps to reproduce the issue, and any error messages shown in the plugin.
What information should I include when contacting RankFrame support?
Include your Framer site URL, the feature that is not working (for example, Submit Indexing or Image SEO), the exact error message or behavior you are seeing, the browser you are using, and whether the issue started after a specific action such as reconnecting Google or publishing a new page.
How long does RankFrame support take to respond?
The 7 Seers team typically responds within one business day. You can check whether the team is currently online by looking for the green support dot in the RankFrame sidebar. When the dot is green, responses are often faster.
Can I fix most RankFrame issues myself without contacting support?
Yes. The majority of RankFrame issues resolve by reconnecting Google, recrawling the site with the Recrawl Website button, checking that toggles are enabled, or upgrading from a trial account. The eight categories above cover the most common scenarios with direct fix instructions for each.
