Google Account Setup

The Setup tab in RankFrame's Submit Indexing section prepares your Google account connection and domain verification. There are two steps. Both are handled automatically once you authorize RankFrame.

Overview

Before RankFrame can push URLs to Google using the official Indexing API, you need to complete the Setup tab. Setup links your Google account to the plugin and confirms that you have verified access to your Framer site's property in Google Search Console.

The Setup tab contains two steps. Both run automatically after you authorize your Google account. You do not need to log into Search Console, create a property manually, add a verification meta tag, or configure any DNS records. RankFrame handles all of it on your behalf.

Tip

Setup only needs to be completed once. Your connection persists across sessions unless you choose to disconnect or revoke access.

The two Setup fields

The Setup tab shows two numbered steps. Both are automatic after you complete the Google OAuth authorization in Step 1.

  • 1
    Connect Google Account
    Authorize RankFrame to access your Google Search Console through Google's standard OAuth flow. Once connected, your account email appears in the Setup tab with a Disconnect option.
  • 2
    Domain Verification
    RankFrame automatically verifies your domain in Google Search Console. No action required. A green success box appears when verification is complete.

Setup tab fields

1
Connect Google Account

This step authorizes RankFrame to access your Google Search Console. What you see depends on whether a Google account is already connected to the plugin.

Setup tab, Connect Google Account section

Path A: No account connected yet

The Setup tab shows a connection screen. Click the button to start the Google OAuth flow. A Google sign-in window opens. Choose your Google account and grant RankFrame permission to access Google Search Console. After you approve, the window closes and the Setup tab updates to show your connected account.

Path B: Account already connected

The Setup tab shows your connected email address (for example, 7seersmedia@gmail.com) alongside the Google G logo. This confirms that RankFrame is actively authorized. A Disconnect option appears in red text below the email address.

Connected state appearance

Google G logo + connected email (e.g. 7seersmedia@gmail.com) + Disconnect in red text

Note

Click Disconnect only if you want to switch Google accounts. Disconnecting removes RankFrame's access token. You will need to go through the OAuth flow again to restore URL submission.

2
Domain Verification

This step verifies that RankFrame has access to your site as a property in Google Search Console. It is completely automatic. RankFrame handles everything on your behalf.

Once your Google account is connected in Step 1, RankFrame runs domain verification automatically. You do not need to add a verification meta tag, upload an HTML file, or create any DNS records.

When verification succeeds, you will see a green success box:

Verified

You have verified access to this property in Search Console. No further action needed!

Once the green box appears, Setup is fully complete. You can move to the Submit URLs tab and start pushing pages to Google.

Setup tab, Domain Verification success state
Tip

If the green box does not appear after connecting your account, wait a few seconds and refresh the Setup tab. Verification typically completes within moments of the Google account connection.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to set up Google Search Console manually before connecting RankFrame?
No. RankFrame handles domain verification automatically on your behalf. You do not need to create a Search Console property, add a verification meta tag, or configure any DNS records before starting. Connect your Google account and RankFrame takes care of the rest.
What does RankFrame access when I connect my Google account?
RankFrame requests access to Google Search Console through a standard Google OAuth flow. This allows the plugin to verify your domain and submit URLs to the Google Indexing API using your credentials. RankFrame does not access Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service.
How do I know if my Google account is already connected in RankFrame?
Open the Setup tab inside the Submit Indexing section. If an account is connected, you will see your email address displayed alongside the Google G logo, with a Disconnect option in red text below it. If no account is connected, you will see a connection screen instead.
Can I disconnect my Google account from RankFrame?
Yes. When your account is connected, the Setup tab shows a Disconnect option in red text below your email. Click it to remove the connection. You can reconnect at any time by going through the Google OAuth flow again.
What does the green success box in Domain Verification mean?
The green box reading "You have verified access to this property in Search Console. No further action needed!" confirms that RankFrame has successfully verified your domain with Google Search Console. Once you see it, Setup is fully complete and you can begin submitting pages for indexing.
