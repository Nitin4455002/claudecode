Google Account Setup

Before you can submit pages for indexing, RankFrame needs a connected Google account and verified access to your Search Console property. Both steps are handled automatically inside the Setup tab.

Overview

The Setup tab inside the Submit Indexing section contains two steps. You complete them once. After that, RankFrame can push any page on your Framer site directly to Google using the official Google Indexing API.

Both steps are automated. RankFrame handles the Google Search Console property verification on your behalf. You do not need to add a meta tag, create a DNS record, or log into Search Console manually.

Tip

Use the Google account that already has access to your site's Search Console property. If your site is not yet in Search Console, connect any Google account and RankFrame will handle the property creation and verification for you.

Setup tab fields

1
Connect Google Account

This step links your Google account to RankFrame so the plugin can communicate with Google Search Console and the Indexing API on your behalf.

There are two possible states for this step.

If no account is connected

RankFrame shows a connection screen. Click the button to start a standard Google OAuth flow. A Google sign-in window opens where you authorize RankFrame to access your Google Search Console. After you approve, the plugin returns to the Setup tab with your account connected.

If an account is already connected

RankFrame shows the connected email address (for example, yourname@gmail.com) alongside the Google G logo. A Disconnect option appears in red below the email. Click Disconnect only if you want to switch to a different Google account. You can reconnect at any time.

Connect Google Account (both states)
Note

RankFrame only requests access to Google Search Console. It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service.

2
Domain Verification

This step verifies that RankFrame has access to your site's property in Google Search Console. It is completely automatic.

Once your Google account is connected, RankFrame verifies your domain in the background. You do not need to add a verification meta tag, upload an HTML file, or create any DNS records. The plugin manages the entire verification process for you.

When verification is complete, you will see a green success box:

Verified

You have verified access to this property in Search Console. No further action needed!

Once you see this message, the Setup tab is fully complete. You can move to the Submit tab and start pushing pages to Google immediately.

Domain Verification (success state)
Tip

If the green success box does not appear after connecting your account, try disconnecting and reconnecting your Google account. Make sure the account you connect has owner-level access to the relevant Search Console property.

Frequently asked questions

Which Google account should I connect to RankFrame?
Connect the Google account that owns or has full access to the Google Search Console property for your Framer site. If you are not sure which account that is, open Search Console at search.google.com/search-console and check which account has your site listed as a verified property.
Does RankFrame create a Search Console property on my behalf?
RankFrame handles domain verification automatically through the Setup tab. You do not need to manually add a property, place a verification meta tag, or create a DNS record. The plugin completes this process for you after you connect your Google account.
What permissions does RankFrame request from Google?
RankFrame requests access to Google Search Console so it can verify your property and use the Google Indexing API on your behalf. This is a standard OAuth flow. RankFrame does not access your Gmail, Drive, or any other Google service.
Can I disconnect my Google account later?
Yes. When your account is connected, the Setup tab shows a Disconnect option in red. Clicking it removes the connection. You can reconnect at any time by going through the same Google OAuth flow.
What does the green success box in the Domain Verification step mean?
The green box that reads "You have verified access to this property in Search Console. No further action needed!" confirms that RankFrame has successfully verified your domain with Google Search Console. Once you see this message, your account is fully configured and you can start submitting pages for indexing.
