Instant Indexing Overview

What is Instant Indexing

Instant Indexing is RankFrame's built-in tool for communicating directly with Google through the official Google Indexing API. Instead of waiting for Googlebot to discover and crawl your pages on its own schedule, you send a direct notification to Google that a URL is ready to be indexed or re-indexed.

The feature lives in the Submit Indexing section of RankFrame, marked by the lightning bolt icon in the left sidebar. At the top of the section you will see the Instant Indexing header alongside a Powered by Google badge. The subtitle reads: "Manage search presence securely via the official Google API."

Tip

Because requests go through the official Google API, every submission is tied to your verified Google account credentials. Google treats these signals as authoritative.

How to open Submit Indexing

Opening the section takes one click.

  • 1
    Click the lightning bolt icon
    Find it in the RankFrame left sidebar. It is the dedicated icon for the Submit Indexing section.
  • 2
    The Instant Indexing panel opens
    You will see the Instant Indexing header, the Powered by Google badge, and the five tabs along the top.
  • 3
    Choose a tab to get started
    If this is your first time, go to the Setup tab first to connect your Google account before submitting any URLs.

The five tabs

The Submit Indexing section is organized into five tabs. Each tab handles a distinct part of the indexing workflow.

Submit URLs
The primary workspace. Enter one or more URLs from your Framer site and send them to Google via the Indexing API with a single action.
Setup
Connect your Google account and authorize RankFrame to use the Indexing API on your behalf. You must complete this step before submitting any URLs.
Analytics
Review performance data for your submitted URLs. Track impressions, clicks, and index coverage changes over time.
Keyword Tracker
Monitor how specific keywords rank for pages you have submitted. See position changes alongside your indexing activity.
Indexing History
A full log of every URL you have submitted, including submission date, API response status, and whether the request was to index or remove the URL.

Before you start

You need to complete the Setup tab before the Submit URLs tab can send requests to Google. The setup process links your Google account to RankFrame and grants the necessary API permissions.

Important

If you skip Setup, the Submit URLs tab will not be able to communicate with the Google Indexing API. Complete Setup first, then return to Submit URLs.

After connecting your account, you are ready to use all five tabs. The connection persists across sessions, so you only need to complete Setup once unless you revoke access or switch accounts.

Why instant indexing matters

Standard search indexing relies on Googlebot crawling your site on its own schedule. For a new or updated Framer site, that wait can range from a few days to several weeks. Your pages may not appear in search results during that window.

Instant Indexing changes that dynamic in two key ways:

  • Direct signal to Google: The Indexing API sends an explicit request to index a specific URL, rather than relying on Googlebot to discover it organically.
  • Re-indexing for updated pages: When you update an existing page, such as a revised blog post or a new product page, you can submit it again so Google picks up the changes faster.

For Framer designers who publish frequently or launch new projects on deadlines, this control is valuable. You publish, you submit, and Google processes the request without an indefinite wait.

Tip

Use the Indexing History tab to confirm that each submission received a successful API response. A successful response means Google acknowledged the request, not necessarily that the page is indexed yet. Check Analytics for coverage confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

Does RankFrame use the official Google Indexing API?
Yes. RankFrame's Instant Indexing feature connects directly to the official Google Indexing API. You authenticate with your own Google account in the Setup tab, so all requests go through your verified credentials.
Do I need to complete Setup before I can submit URLs?
Yes. You must connect your Google account in the Setup tab first. Without a valid connection, the Submit URLs tab cannot send requests to the Google Indexing API.
How is Instant Indexing different from waiting for Googlebot to crawl my site?
Standard crawling relies on Googlebot discovering and visiting your pages on its own schedule, which can take days or weeks. Instant Indexing sends a direct signal to Google via the official API, telling it to index or re-index a specific URL right away.
What does the Indexing History tab show?
The Indexing History tab shows a log of every URL you have submitted through RankFrame, including the submission date, the status returned by the Google Indexing API, and whether the request was an index or remove action.
Where do I find the Submit Indexing section in RankFrame?
Click the lightning bolt icon in the RankFrame left sidebar. The Submit Indexing section opens with the Instant Indexing header and the five tabs: Submit URLs, Setup, Analytics, Keyword Tracker, and Indexing History.
