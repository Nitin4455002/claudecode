Keyword Tracker Overview

The Keyword Tracker in RankFrame lets you monitor how specific search terms are performing for your Framer site over time. Unlike the Analytics tab (which shows aggregate traffic data), the Keyword Tracker is keyword-by-keyword: you choose the terms that matter to your business and watch their position, clicks, and impressions update automatically as Google updates its index. All data comes directly from Google Search Console through the same verified connection you set up for indexing.

How the Keyword Tracker Works

1 Where to Find the Keyword Tracker

Open Submit Indexing by clicking the lightning bolt icon in the left sidebar of RankFrame. Then click the Keyword Tracker tab at the top of the panel. It is the fourth tab across: Submit URLs | Setup | Analytics | Keyword Tracker | Indexing History.

Keyword Tracker tab selected inside the Submit Indexing panel
2 What the Keyword Tracker Does

The Keyword Tracker stores a list of keywords you define and shows their current Google Search Console metrics in a structured table. The three core columns are:

Impressions: how many times your pages appeared in Google results for that query. Clicks: how many times a searcher clicked through to your site. Search Position: your average ranking position for the keyword. A lower number is better. Position 1 means your page appears first in results.

3 Position History Sparkline

The rightmost column of the keyword table shows a small sparkline chart for each keyword. The sparkline visualizes position movement over time so you can see at a glance whether a keyword is trending up, down, or holding steady.

When a position improves, a green up arrow and a numeric delta appear next to the current position number (for example: "2.4" with an up arrow and "0.3"). This lets you confirm ranking movement is real and directional, without leaving the RankFrame panel or opening an external tool.

Close-up of Position History sparkline with green up arrow and numeric delta
4 Where the Data Comes From

The Keyword Tracker pulls data from Google Search Console via the same OAuth connection you established in the Submit Indexing Setup tab. If the connection is active and your property is verified, data refreshes automatically as Search Console updates. New data typically appears within 48 to 72 hours. You do not need a separate Search Console login, a separate API key, or a third-party rank-tracking subscription.

Submit Indexing Setup tab showing an active Google Search Console connection
5 Keyword Manager Search

Above the keyword table, the Keyword Manager includes a search field. Type any string to filter your tracked keywords in real time. Only rows matching your query remain visible, making it fast to find and review a specific term once your list grows to dozens of entries.

Keyword Manager search field actively filtering the keyword table
Note: The Keyword Tracker shows data Google has already reported for your site. Position history reflects past averages, not real-time ranking snapshots. Expect 48 to 72 hours for new keyword data to appear after adding a keyword to the tracker.

Frequently Asked Questions

You need the Google account connected in the Submit Indexing Setup tab and your domain verified in Google Search Console. If Submit Indexing is already working for you, the Keyword Tracker uses the same connection with no extra setup required.

Analytics shows aggregate site-level data: total clicks, total impressions, average CTR, and average position across all queries. The Keyword Tracker is keyword-specific: you define which terms to monitor and the tracker shows per-keyword metrics and trend history for each one individually.

There is no documented hard limit. Keep the list focused on your core business terms for the clearest picture of SEO progress. Tracking too many terms at once can make the table harder to review on a regular basis.

Google Search Console averages position across all impressions for that keyword. If your page appears as position 2 for some searches and position 3 for others in the same reporting period, the reported average is 2.5. Decimal positions are normal and expected for any keyword with a meaningful number of impressions.

The Keyword Tracker shows sitewide performance for each keyword across your entire Framer site. To identify which specific page is ranking for a term, cross-reference with the Analytics tab's page drill-down view.
