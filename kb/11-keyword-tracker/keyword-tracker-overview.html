Keyword Tracker Overview

The Keyword Tracker in RankFrame lets you monitor how specific search terms are performing for your Framer site over time. Unlike the Analytics tab (which shows aggregate traffic data), the Keyword Tracker is keyword-by-keyword: you choose the terms that matter to your business and watch their position, clicks, and impressions update automatically as Google updates its index. All data comes directly from Google Search Console through the same verified connection you set up for indexing.

1
Where to Find the Keyword Tracker
Open Submit Indexing by clicking the lightning bolt icon in the left sidebar, then click the Keyword Tracker tab. It is the fourth tab across the top row: Submit URLs | Setup | Analytics | Keyword Tracker | Indexing History. The tab is available as long as your Google Search Console connection is active.
Keyword Tracker tab highlighted inside the Submit Indexing panel
2
What the Keyword Tracker Does
The Keyword Tracker stores a list of keywords you care about and shows their current Google Search Console metrics for your Framer site.
Keyword Manager table showing sample keywords with Impressions, Clicks, and Position columns
Column What it means
Keyword The search term you added to the tracker.
Impressions How many times your pages appeared in Google results for this query.
Clicks How many times searchers clicked through to your site.
Search Position Your average ranking position. A lower number is better: position 1 is first place.
Position History A sparkline mini chart showing position movement over time.
3
Position History Sparkline
The rightmost column of the keyword table shows a small sparkline chart for each keyword. The sparkline visualizes position movement over time. When your position improves, a green up arrow and a numeric delta appear next to the current position (for example: 2.4 ↑ 0.3). This lets you see ranking trends at a glance without opening any external tool or switching to Google Search Console directly.
Position History column with sparkline and green up arrow indicator visible
4
Where the Data Comes From
The Keyword Tracker pulls data from Google Search Console via the same OAuth connection you set up in the Submit Indexing Setup tab. If the connection is active and your property is verified, data updates automatically as Search Console refreshes. You do not need a separate Search Console login or a separate SEO tool. RankFrame handles the data pipeline for you inside Framer.
Submit Indexing Setup tab showing active Google Search Console connection status
ⓘ
The Keyword Tracker shows data Google has reported for your site. Position history reflects past averages, not real-time ranking snapshots. Expect 48 to 72 hours for new keyword data to appear after adding a keyword.
5
Keyword Manager Search
Above the keyword table, the Keyword Manager includes a search field. Type any string to filter the keyword list in real time. Useful when you have tracked dozens of keywords and want to jump to a specific term quickly without scrolling through the full table.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • You need the Google account connected in the Submit Indexing Setup tab and your domain verified in Search Console. If Submit Indexing is already working for you, the Keyword Tracker uses the same connection with no extra setup required.
  • Analytics shows aggregate site-level data: total clicks, total impressions, average CTR, and average position across all queries. The Keyword Tracker is keyword-specific. You define which terms to monitor and the tracker shows per-keyword metrics and trend history for each one individually.
  • There is no documented hard limit. Keep the list focused on your core business terms for the clearest picture of SEO progress. A focused list of 20 to 50 keywords is easier to act on than hundreds of loosely related terms.
  • Google Search Console averages position across all impressions for that keyword. If your page appears as position 2 for some searches and position 3 for others in the same period, the average is 2.5. Decimals are normal and expected in all Search Console position reporting.
  • The Keyword Tracker shows sitewide performance for each keyword. To see which specific page is ranking for a term, cross-reference with the Analytics tab's Page drill-down, which breaks down metrics by URL.
