What is the SEO Analyzer?

The SEO Analyzer is a site-wide audit tool built into RankFrame. It scans every published page of your Framer site and returns a single score plus a categorized list of issues.

It is different from the per-page audit inside the Pages section. The per-page audit focuses on one URL at a time. The SEO Analyzer gives you the full picture across your entire site so you can prioritize the fixes that matter most.

Note

The SEO Analyzer scans your live, published site. Make sure your latest Framer publish is live before running or refreshing an audit.

Opening the Analyzer

Open RankFrame inside Framer. Look for the bar chart icon in the left sidebar. Click it to open the SEO Analyzer. The subtitle beneath the section name reads "Analyze your website SEO."

Header Controls

Three controls appear at the top of the SEO Analyzer:

  • Live SEO indicator - A green dot that confirms the analyzer is connected and reflecting live site data.
  • Refresh Analysis button - Re-runs the audit against your published site and updates all scores and issue lists.
  • Download Audit button - Shown in purple. Exports your full audit report as a PDF you can save or share.
Tip

Click Download Audit before and after a round of fixes to compare PDF snapshots and show progress to clients.

Your SEO Score

The score panel dominates the top of the audit view. It shows a large circular score (for example, 82/100) surrounded by a purple ring. Below the ring you will see the Last Checked date and your full site URL.

The score is a composite number that weighs critical failures, warnings, passing checks, and speed. A higher score means your site is in better SEO shape. Aim to clear all Red Zone items first, then work through Warnings.

Score Breakdown

Below the circular score you will find four summary counts that explain where your score comes from:

  • Red Zone - Critical failures. These have the most negative impact on rankings.
  • Warnings - Issues that need attention but are not blocking. Fix these after clearing the Red Zone.
  • Green Zone - Passing checks. These confirm what your site is already doing right.
  • Speed - Your site load time in milliseconds. Lower is better.

Filter Tabs

Five filter tabs sit above the issue list. Use them to focus on one category at a time instead of scrolling through every check at once.

All
Shows every check from every category in a single combined list. Good for a full overview before you start prioritizing.
Green Zone
Filters to checks your site is already passing. Review these to understand what is working so you do not accidentally break it.
Red Zone
Shows only critical failures. Start here. These items drag your score down the most and should be fixed first.
Warnings
Lists issues that are not critical but still hurt your SEO. Address these after clearing all Red Zone failures.
Speed
A dedicated tab with separate Mobile and Desktop performance gauges and your full Core Web Vitals breakdown. Treat this as its own sub-report.
Pro Tip

Switch to the Red Zone tab immediately after an audit. Fix those items, click Refresh Analysis, then move to the Warnings tab. This order gives you the fastest score improvement.

Clicking into Issues

Every check item in the list is clickable. The sidebar tip panel in the SEO Analyzer reminds you of this directly:

SEO Analyzer Tip

Pro Tip: Click any issue in the analyzer to see detailed suggestions.

When you click an issue, RankFrame opens a detailed panel with an explanation of the problem, why it matters for SEO, and specific suggestions for fixing it inside Framer. Use this detail view to understand each check before making changes.

  • Click any Red Zone item to see exactly what is failing and how to resolve it.
  • Click any Warning item for guidance on the recommended improvement.
  • Click any Green Zone item to confirm what the check validates.
  • Click any Speed metric to see what it measures and how to improve it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the SEO Analyzer and the per-page audit?
The SEO Analyzer runs a site-wide audit across every page of your Framer site. The per-page audit, found inside the Pages section, focuses on one specific page at a time. Use the SEO Analyzer for a full health check and the per-page audit for targeted improvements on individual URLs.
How do I refresh my SEO audit?
Click the Refresh Analysis button in the top area of the SEO Analyzer header. RankFrame will re-crawl your published site and update your score and issue list with the latest data.
What does the Red Zone mean in the score breakdown?
The Red Zone shows critical SEO failures that have the most negative impact on your rankings. These are high-priority items you should fix before addressing Warnings or reviewing Green Zone passes.
How do I export my SEO audit as a PDF?
Click the Download Audit button (shown in purple) in the SEO Analyzer header. RankFrame generates a PDF report you can save locally or share with clients.
What does the Speed tab show?
The Speed tab displays separate Mobile and Desktop performance gauges alongside your Core Web Vitals metrics. These scores reflect how quickly your site loads for visitors on each device type and help you identify which performance improvements will have the biggest impact.
