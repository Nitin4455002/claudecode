Understanding the Audit Score Panel

The Audit Score Panel gives you an at-a-glance health summary for your Framer site. This article explains every element in the panel and shows you how to use the filter tabs to focus on specific check categories.

Panel overview

When you run an SEO audit in RankFrame, the top of the results view displays the Score Panel. It contains the date the audit last ran, the full URL of your site, the overall score, a breakdown of check results by zone, and a set of filter tabs that control which checks appear in the list below.

On the right side of the panel you will see a brand pill reading "RankFrame, Best Framer SEO Plugin" and a target label reading "Good SEO. Target: 90+" to remind you of the benchmark to aim for.

Score Panel overview screenshot
Tip

Run your audit regularly after publishing new pages or updating site content. The Last Checked date in the panel tells you exactly when the most recent scan was performed.

1 Circular Score

The large circle in the center of the panel displays your overall SEO score as a number from 0 to 100, surrounded by a purple ring. The ring fills proportionally to your score so you can gauge health at a glance.

The score is calculated from the combined weight of all passing checks, warnings, and critical fails across Basic SEO, Advanced SEO, Performance, and Security categories. A higher ratio of passing checks produces a higher score.

  • Scores of 90 and above indicate good SEO health.
  • Scores between 70 and 89 suggest notable issues that should be addressed soon.
  • Scores below 70 mean significant problems that need immediate attention.

2 Red Zone count

The Red Zone count shows how many checks have returned a critical failure. A red dot appears next to the count to distinguish it from the other zones.

Critical fails have the highest negative impact on your search rankings. Prioritize fixing every Red Zone issue before addressing warnings or optimizing other areas.

Important

Even a single Red Zone failure, such as a missing canonical tag or a broken sitemap, can significantly reduce your site's crawlability and indexing. Address these first.

3 Warnings count

The Warnings count shows how many checks have returned a non-critical issue. Warnings are not as urgent as Red Zone failures, but they still affect your overall score and should be resolved.

Common warning scenarios include suboptimal image sizes, missing keywords in descriptions, or pages that lack OpenGraph meta tags. Resolving warnings after all critical fails are cleared is the recommended approach.

Warnings count indicator screenshot
4 Green Zone count

The Green Zone count shows how many checks are passing. A green dot marks this figure. You do not need to take action on Green Zone items.

Use this number to track progress over time. As you fix Red Zone issues and resolve warnings, your Green Zone count should rise and your overall score should increase toward the 90+ target.

Green Zone count indicator screenshot
5 Speed

The Speed metric displays your server response time in milliseconds. A fast response time is a direct ranking signal for search engines and affects user experience.

Lower response times are better. If the value shown is high, switch to the Speed filter tab (described below) to review the individual performance checks and identify what is causing the slowdown.

  • Under 200 ms: excellent.
  • 200 to 500 ms: acceptable, with room to improve.
  • Above 500 ms: should be investigated and optimized.
Speed metric screenshot
6 Filter tabs

Below the score breakdown, five filter tabs let you narrow the check list to a specific zone: All, Green Zone, Red Zone, Warnings, and Speed.

Clicking a tab instantly filters the check list so that only items matching that category are shown. This makes it easier to work through one type of issue at a time without losing track of where you are.

  • All: shows every check result regardless of status.
  • Green Zone: shows only passing checks.
  • Red Zone: shows only critical failures.
  • Warnings: shows only non-critical issues.
  • Speed: shows only performance-related checks.
Tip

Start with the Red Zone tab to work through your most urgent issues first, then move to Warnings, and finish by reviewing the Speed tab for performance improvements.

Frequently asked questions

What does the circular score number mean?
The circular score is an overall SEO health score from 0 to 100. It is calculated from the ratio of passing checks, warnings, and critical fails across all audit categories. A score of 90 or above is considered good SEO by RankFrame.
What is the target score I should aim for?
RankFrame recommends targeting a score of 90 or higher. The score panel displays the label "Good SEO. Target: 90+" to remind you of this benchmark.
What is the difference between Red Zone and Warnings?
Red Zone checks are critical failures that have the highest impact on your search rankings and should be fixed immediately. Warnings are non-critical issues that are worth addressing but will not cause the same level of harm if left unfixed for a short period.
What does the Speed metric measure?
The Speed metric shows your page's server response time in milliseconds. A fast response time is a positive ranking signal. You can click the Speed filter tab to isolate speed-related checks in the list below the panel.
Why is my score lower after adding new pages?
New pages may introduce missing meta tags, missing ALT attributes, or other issues that the audit detects. Run a fresh audit from the score panel and review the Red Zone and Warnings tabs to identify which pages need attention.
