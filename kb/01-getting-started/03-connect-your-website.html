How to Connect Your Website in RankFrame

Connecting your Framer site to RankFrame is the first step in your SEO setup. This article explains how it works, what RankFrame scans, and the site limits for each plan.

How connecting works

When you open RankFrame in a Framer project for the first time, it asks you to connect your site. Connecting links your Framer project's published URL to your RankFrame account.

This is what allows RankFrame to read your page structure, scan your images, run audits, and push pages to Google on your behalf. Without a connected site, RankFrame cannot access any data about your pages.

Connecting your site does not modify your live Framer site. It is a read-only scan that builds a map of your site inside RankFrame.

Steps to connect your site

  1. 1
    Open RankFrame in your Framer project
    Click Plugins in the Framer toolbar and select RankFrame. If you have not installed it yet, see How to Install RankFrame in Framer.
  2. 2
    Sign in or create your account
    Sign in with your email and password, or use Continue with Google. If this is your first time, your account is created automatically.
  3. 3
    Click Connect Site
    RankFrame shows a Connect Site prompt with your Framer project's URL pre-filled. Confirm the URL is correct and click Connect.
  4. 4
    Wait for the scan to complete
    RankFrame scans your sitemap and image inventory. This takes a few seconds on most sites. Larger sites with hundreds of pages may take up to 30 seconds.
  5. 5
    Complete Personalization
    After connecting, RankFrame asks for your brand details. Fill in your Brand Name, Brand About, goals, industry, audience, and any competitor URLs. Click Save Settings. See Managing Your Account for a full breakdown of each field.
Tip

Your site must be published before RankFrame can connect. If you see a connection error, check that your Framer site is published and accessible at a public URL.

What RankFrame scans

When you connect your site, RankFrame performs an initial scan that reads the following.

  • Your sitemap at /sitemap.xml. This builds the page list you see in the Pages section and the Submit Indexing flow.
  • Canvas images across all static pages. These appear in Image SEO under the Canvas folder.
  • CMS collection images for all connected CMS collections. These appear in Image SEO under the CMS folder, organized by collection name.
  • Page meta tags. RankFrame reads existing titles and descriptions so it can display your current meta alongside AI-generated alternatives.

RankFrame does not read unpublished drafts, private pages, or pages excluded from your sitemap via robots.txt.

How to disconnect a site

The disconnect process depends on which plan you are on.

Agency plan

You can connect and disconnect unlimited sites at any time from inside the plugin. Go to your account settings and manage connected sites from the site selector.

Personal plan

Disconnecting a site on the Personal plan requires emailing hello@7seersmedia.com. The Personal plan binds to the connected site on activation, and the support team handles disconnections manually.

Important

Disconnecting a site removes all RankFrame data associated with it, including audit history, keyword tracking data, and 404 logs. This action cannot be undone.

Frequently asked questions

How many sites can I connect to RankFrame?
The Personal plan includes one connected Framer site. The Agency plan includes unlimited connected sites. Connecting additional sites on the Personal plan requires upgrading to Agency.
Can I switch my connected site on the Personal plan?
Yes, but it requires a manual step. Email hello@7seersmedia.com to request a site disconnect. Once the team processes it, you can connect a different Framer site.
Does connecting my site affect my live Framer site?
No. Connecting your site is a read-only scan. It does not modify your live site in any way. Changes to your live site only happen when you actively push them from inside RankFrame.
My site is still in development and not published. Can I still connect it?
RankFrame needs access to a published or staging URL to scan your page structure. Publish your Framer project first, even to a staging subdomain, then connect it to RankFrame.
What does Recrawl Website do?
Recrawl Website, available from the plugin sidebar, re-runs the site scan. Use it after adding new pages, uploading new images, or making significant changes to your Framer site so RankFrame reflects the latest version. Trial users get 1 recrawl. Paid users get unlimited recrawls.
