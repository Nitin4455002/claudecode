How to Get Support for RankFrame

Several ways to reach the RankFrame team: in-plugin chat, email, and the help center. This article covers all your options and what to include when reaching out.

Check the documentation first

The fastest way to get an answer is often through the help center. Most setup questions, feature explanations, and common issues are covered here.

Open the full documentation from the Documentation link in the RankFrame plugin sidebar. It opens in your browser at rankframe.com/help.

Tip

What to include in your message

The more context you provide, the faster the team can help. Include the following in any support message.

  • Your account email address
  • The Framer project URL or the connected site URL
  • A clear description of what you were trying to do
  • What happened instead of what you expected
  • Any error message you saw (copy the exact text if possible)
  • A screenshot or screen recording if the issue is visual

For billing questions, include your plan name and the approximate date of the charge in question.

Reporting a bug

If you find something broken in RankFrame, the team wants to know. Email hello@7seersmedia.com with the following.

  • What you did: the exact steps that led to the bug
  • What you expected: what should have happened
  • What happened instead: the actual behavior or error
  • Screenshot or recording: especially useful for visual bugs or unexpected UI behavior
  • Browser and OS: Chrome on Mac, Safari on Mac, etc.

The team typically acknowledges bug reports within one business day and ships fixes for critical issues as quickly as possible.

Requesting a feature

RankFrame is built based on real workflow problems. Feature requests from users directly shape what gets shipped next.

To request a feature, email hello@7seersmedia.com with a description of the feature and the problem it would solve for your workflow. Be specific. "I need X so that I can Y" is more useful than "it would be nice to have X."

You can also share feedback on @rankframe or in the Framer Community. The team reads all of it.

Note

RankFrame is built by a small team. Not every request can be shipped immediately, but every message is read and considered in the product roadmap.

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact RankFrame support?
Click the Support link in the RankFrame sidebar for in-plugin chat, or email hello@7seersmedia.com. The team replies within one business day.
What is the response time for RankFrame support?
Email support typically receives a reply within one business day. In-plugin chat is available when the green dot is visible next to Support in the sidebar, indicating the team is online.
Where can I find RankFrame documentation?
The full documentation is at rankframe.com/help. You can also open it directly from the Documentation link in the RankFrame plugin sidebar.
How do I request a new feature for RankFrame?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com with a description of the feature and how you would use it. You can also share feedback on @rankframe on X or in the Framer Community. The team reads all of it.
How do I report a bug in RankFrame?
Email hello@7seersmedia.com. Include your account email, what you were doing, what happened, and a screenshot if possible. This helps the team reproduce and fix the issue faster.
