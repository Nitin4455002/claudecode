Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to begin. You get full access to all features from day one.

Tip

Use the free trial to run a full site audit, check your SEO score, and see exactly which issues need fixing. The trial gives you enough data to decide which plan fits your workflow.

Annual billing

Both plans are available on monthly or annual billing. Annual billing saves 40% on both plans.

  • Personal Annual: $108 per year, equivalent to $9 per month
  • Agency Annual: $288 per year, equivalent to $24 per month

Your renewal date shows in the plugin sidebar (e.g. "Renews in 357 days"). Switch from monthly to annual at any time from the See Billing button in the sidebar.

AI credits

AI credits power every AI generation in RankFrame. Credits are separate from your subscription and are purchased as needed.

Every new account receives a starter credit balance on signup to try AI features during the trial.

What uses credits

  • AI meta title generation (Global Settings and per-page)
  • AI meta description generation (Global Settings and per-page)
  • AI image alt text generation (single image or bulk)
  • AI schema field suggestions (Auto-Generate flow)

1 credit equals 1 AI generation. Generating alt text for 500 images uses 500 credits.

Credit pricing

Credits are purchased from the Buy Credits button in the Image SEO section. Refills are instant. Credits never expire while your subscription is active.

Note

Credits are non-refundable once used. Unused credits remain in your account balance until you use them.

Which plan is right for you

Choose Personal if you

  • Own or manage one Framer site
  • Are a freelance designer handling a single project
  • Run your own business on a single Framer site

Choose Agency if you

  • Manage multiple client Framer sites
  • Run a design studio or agency
  • Want to offer SEO audits and reports to clients (white-label coming)
  • Need to switch between multiple sites inside one account

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free version of RankFrame?
RankFrame does not have a permanently free plan, but every account starts with a 14-day free trial with full access and credit card required. After 14 days, choose a paid plan to continue.
What is the difference between Personal and Agency?
The Personal plan covers one connected Framer site. The Agency plan covers unlimited Framer sites. Both plans include the same full feature set. The Agency plan also includes priority support and white-label PDF reports when they launch.
What are AI credits in RankFrame?
AI credits power every AI feature: meta title and description generation, image alt text generation, and schema field suggestions. Each AI generation uses 1 credit. Credits are purchased separately and never expire while your account is active.
Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?
Yes. Open the See Billing button in the plugin sidebar. From the billing portal, you can switch from monthly to annual at any time and immediately save 40%.
What happens when my free trial ends?
When your 14-day trial ends, RankFrame limits access to paid features. Your data and settings are preserved. Click See Billing in the plugin sidebar to choose a plan and restore full access.
