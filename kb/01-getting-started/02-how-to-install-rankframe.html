How to Install RankFrame in Framer

Add RankFrame to any Framer project in under 2 minutes. No coding required. No separate account setup before installing.

Before you start

You need two things before installing RankFrame.

  • A Framer account. RankFrame is a Framer-native plugin and lives inside the Framer editor.
  • A Framer project connected to a published or staging site. RankFrame reads your site's page structure on first load.

You do not need a RankFrame account before installing. Your account is created automatically on first launch.

Installation steps

Framer plugins do not require a download or installation file. You open them directly from inside Framer.

  1. 1
    Open your Framer project
    Go to framer.com and open the project you want to add RankFrame to. You need to be inside the Framer editor, not the dashboard.
  2. 2
    Click the Plugins button
    Find the Plugins button in the Framer toolbar. It appears in the top bar of the editor. Click it to open the Plugin panel.
  3. 3
    Search for RankFrame
    Type RankFrame in the search field inside the Plugin panel. The RankFrame plugin appears in the results. You can also browse to the SEO category to find it.
  4. 4
    Open RankFrame
    Click RankFrame in the results. The plugin opens immediately in your project as a side panel. There is no separate install confirmation step.
  5. 5
    Create your account or sign in
    If this is your first time, RankFrame shows a sign-up screen. Enter your email and set a password, or continue with Google Auth. Your account is created instantly.
  6. 6
    Connect your site
    After signing in, RankFrame asks you to connect the Framer site for this project. Click Connect Site. RankFrame scans your site structure and loads your pages automatically.
Tip

You can also find RankFrame on the Framer Plugin Marketplace. Click Open plugin in and choose your project from the dropdown.

What happens on first launch

The first time RankFrame loads in your project, it shows the Personalization setup screen. This is not optional. It runs before any AI feature becomes available.

Personalization asks for your brand name, a description of what your business does, your main goals, industry, target audience, and any competitor URLs you want RankFrame to be aware of.

This data feeds every AI output in RankFrame: meta titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and schema field suggestions. Without it, AI generates generic copy. With it, AI writes copy that sounds like your brand.

Note

Personalization data is saved to your account, not your Framer project. If you open RankFrame in a different project, your Personalization settings carry over automatically.

Troubleshooting

RankFrame does not appear in search results

Make sure you are searching inside the Framer Plugin panel, not the Framer asset or component search. The Plugin panel opens from the Plugins button in the top toolbar. If RankFrame still does not appear, try searching for "7 Seers" instead.

The plugin opens but does not load

Refresh the Framer editor by pressing Command + R (Mac) or Ctrl + R (Windows). If the issue persists, check that your browser allows third-party cookies. RankFrame uses cookies for authentication.

The site connection step fails

RankFrame needs access to your published Framer site to scan your page structure. If you have not published your project yet, publish it first from the Framer toolbar, then re-open RankFrame.

Something else is wrong

Contact support at hello@7seersmedia.com. Include your Framer project URL and a screenshot of the error if possible.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find RankFrame in Framer?
Click the Plugins button in the Framer toolbar, then search for RankFrame. You can also find it on the Framer Plugin Marketplace at framer.com/marketplace and open it directly into your project from there.
Do I need to be a developer to install RankFrame?
No. RankFrame installs and runs entirely through the Framer plugin panel. No code, no terminal, no configuration files required.
Can I use RankFrame on multiple Framer projects?
The plugin is accessible in any Framer project. However, one connected site is included in the Personal plan. The Agency plan covers unlimited connected Framer sites. See Plans and Pricing for full details.
Does installing RankFrame change my live Framer site?
Opening RankFrame in Framer does not change your live site. RankFrame only affects your live site when you actively push changes, such as injecting schema or submitting pages to Google via the Indexing API.
Is RankFrame available for Framer desktop app users?
Yes. RankFrame works in the Framer web editor and the Framer desktop app. The plugin panel behaves the same in both.
