What restaurant schema does

Restaurant schema is a subtype of LocalBusiness. It tells Google the essential details about a food service establishment: who they are, where they are, when they are open, what they serve, and how much it costs. Google uses this data to populate local search results, the local map pack, and knowledge panels.

For any Framer site representing a restaurant, cafe, bakery, or food business, this schema type is essential for local SEO visibility.

Name, Description, Phone

Basic Info
Field What to enter
Name The official name of the restaurant exactly as it appears on signage and branding (e.g. "The Blue Spoon"). Becomes the name property.
Description A 1 to 3 sentence description of the restaurant. Describe the cuisine, atmosphere, or what makes this place unique. Becomes the description property.
Phone The restaurant's primary phone number including country code (e.g. +1-555-234-5678). Becomes the telephone property.

Address

Address

The Address section maps directly to the address object in the JSON-LD, using the PostalAddress type. Fill every field that applies to your location.

Field What to enter
Street The street address including building number (e.g. "42 Main Street, Suite 3"). Becomes address.streetAddress.
City The city or locality (e.g. "Brooklyn"). Becomes address.addressLocality.
State The state, province, or region (e.g. "NY" or "New York"). Becomes address.addressRegion.
Postal Code The ZIP code or postal code (e.g. "11201"). Becomes address.postalCode.
Country The two-letter ISO country code (e.g. "US" for United States, "IN" for India, "GB" for United Kingdom). Becomes address.addressCountry.
Tip

Accurate address data is critical for Google Maps integration. Make sure the address in your schema exactly matches the address on your Google Business Profile and other directory listings.

Cuisine, Menu URL, Price Range

Restaurant Details
Field What to enter
Cuisine The type of cuisine served (e.g. "Italian", "Japanese", "Mediterranean", "Indian"). You can list multiple cuisine types separated by commas. Becomes the servesCuisine property.
Menu URL A direct URL to the online menu page (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/menu). Becomes the hasMenu property. Leave empty if no online menu exists.
Price Range The relative price level using dollar signs: $ for inexpensive, $$ for moderate, $$$ for expensive, $$$$ for very expensive. Becomes the priceRange property.

Working Hours

Working Hours

The Working Hours section lets you define opening hours for each day of the week. Each day has an individual toggle to mark it as open or closed. For days the restaurant is open, you add one or more time slots (shifts).

Day toggles

Seven days appear: Monday through Sunday. Toggle each day on to mark it as a day the restaurant is open. Days left toggled off are treated as closed and excluded from the openingHoursSpecification in the JSON-LD.

Time slots and multi-shift support

For each day that is toggled on, you can add time slots. Each time slot has two fields:

Field What to enter
Opens The opening time for this shift in 24-hour format (e.g. 09:00 for 9 AM, 18:00 for 6 PM). Becomes openingHoursSpecification.opens.
Closes The closing time for this shift in 24-hour format (e.g. 15:00 for 3 PM, 22:30 for 10:30 PM). Becomes openingHoursSpecification.closes.

To add a second shift for the same day (for example, a lunch service and a dinner service), add a second time slot row for that day. Each shift is a separate openingHoursSpecification entry in the JSON-LD.

Example

A restaurant open for lunch 12:00-15:00 and dinner 18:00-22:00 on Fridays would have two rows for Friday: one with Opens 12:00 Closes 15:00, and a second with Opens 18:00 Closes 22:00.

Saving and injecting

  1. Click the Preview JSON tab to review the complete JSON-LD including the address object and opening hours specification.
  2. Click Validator (Ext) to validate against Google Rich Results Test.
  3. Click Copy JSON to copy the raw JSON-LD if needed.
  4. Click Save Schema to save to Saved Schemas.
  5. Toggle the schema on, then click Save and Inject.

Frequently asked questions

What does Restaurant schema do for SEO?
It tells Google the business name, address, phone, cuisine, menu, price range, and opening hours. This data populates Google Maps, local pack results, and knowledge panels, significantly improving local SEO visibility.
How do I add multiple shifts for one day?
Toggle the day on in Working Hours, then add multiple time slot rows for that day. Each row with Opens and Closes times represents one shift. For a restaurant open for lunch and dinner, add two rows for each relevant day.
What format should I use for Price Range?
Use dollar sign notation: $ for inexpensive, $$ for moderate, $$$ for expensive, $$$$ for very expensive. This maps to the priceRange property in JSON-LD.
Is Restaurant schema only for restaurants?
The Restaurant type specifically represents food service businesses. For other local businesses, the Service schema type in RankFrame is more appropriate.
