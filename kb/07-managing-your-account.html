Opening Account Settings

Click the account block at the bottom of the RankFrame sidebar. This is the block that shows your avatar, name, and email. A modal opens with two tabs: Profile and Personalization.

Profile tab

The Profile tab shows your account details.

  • Avatar: your initials in a purple circle
  • Name: the name on your account
  • Plan badge: your current plan (Free Trial, Personal, or Agency)
  • Email Address: the email you signed up with
  • Joined Date: when you created your RankFrame account
  • Status: your authentication method (Google Auth or Email + Password)

All profile fields are read-only inside the plugin. To update your name, email, or password, visit dashboard.rankframe.com.

Note

If you signed up with Google Auth, your name and email come from your Google account. Update them from your Google account settings to see changes reflected in RankFrame.

Personalization tab

The Personalization tab is the most important account setting in RankFrame. It contains the brand context that every AI feature reads before generating any output.

Without Personalization data, AI writes generic, template-sounding copy. With it, AI generates titles, descriptions, alt text, and schema fields that sound like your brand.

The Personalization tab has two sections: Brand Identity and Strategy.

Brand Identity fields

  • Brand Name The name of the business or project. This appears in AI-generated meta titles, schema markup, and alt text. Example: 7 Seers
  • Brand About A multi-line description of what the business does, who it serves, and what makes it different. Be specific. The more context you provide, the more relevant every AI output becomes. Example: "7 Seers is a design and software studio from Lucknow, India. We build Framer websites and custom software for SaaS founders, agencies, and premium brands."

Strategy fields

  • Main Goals Multi-select dropdown. Choose all goals that apply to your site. Options include Increase Traffic, Generate Leads, Boost Sales, Brand Awareness, Local Presence, and Improve Authority. RankFrame uses these to prioritize the angle of AI-written copy.
  • Industry Tag input. Type your industry and press Enter. You can add multiple tags. Common examples include SaaS, Agency, E-commerce, Portfolio, Local Business, and Startup.
  • Audience Tag input. Describe who your site is targeting. Add multiple audience tags. Examples: SaaS Founders, Business Owners, Freelance Designers, Marketing Teams.
  • Competitors (URLs) Add URLs of competitor sites, one per line. RankFrame uses these to understand your competitive landscape and write copy that positions you clearly against them. Click + Add Competitor to add a URL. Example: https://competitor.com

After filling in all fields, click Save Settings. Your Personalization data is applied immediately to all subsequent AI generations.

Tip

Update your Personalization data whenever your business focus shifts, you launch a new product, or you start managing a new client site. The quality of AI output is directly tied to the quality of this context.

Signing out

To sign out of RankFrame, click the account block in the sidebar to open Account Settings. Switch to the Profile tab and click the Sign Out button at the bottom of the modal.

Signing out removes your session from the plugin. The next time you open RankFrame in Framer, you will be prompted to sign back in.

Note

Signing out does not delete your account or data. All your settings, audit history, and Personalization data are saved and available when you sign back in.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change my email address in RankFrame?
Profile details including your name, email, and password are read-only inside the plugin. To update them, visit dashboard.rankframe.com and make changes from your account settings there.
What is Personalization in RankFrame?
Personalization is the brand context setup that powers every AI feature in RankFrame. It includes your brand name, a description of your business, your goals, industry, audience, and competitor URLs. Without it, AI generates generic copy. With it, AI writes copy that sounds like your brand.
Does Personalization data carry over between Framer projects?
Yes. Personalization data is saved to your RankFrame account, not your Framer project. If you open RankFrame in a different project, your Personalization settings are already there.
How do I sign out of RankFrame?
Click the account block at the bottom of the sidebar to open Account Settings. Switch to the Profile tab and click the Sign Out button at the bottom.
Can I have separate Personalization settings for different client sites?
Currently, Personalization is set once per account. If you manage multiple client sites on the Agency plan, set Personalization to reflect your agency identity, or update it when switching between clients to get the most relevant AI output for each.
